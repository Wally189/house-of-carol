How the House works

Ideas are cheap. Evidence earns the next move.

House of Carol uses a portfolio approach: observe a problem or opportunity, understand it, test the smallest credible proposition, then operate, expand, change or stop according to what reality shows.

The venture discipline

Four movements keep the House grounded.

Observe

Start with something real: a recurring problem, neglected customer need, inefficient process, useful audience, underused asset or commercial opportunity.

Research

Test assumptions against customers, markets, alternatives, law, delivery constraints and economics. An attractive idea is not yet evidence of demand.

Test

Develop the smallest competent proposition capable of answering the important uncertainty. Avoid building the elaborate version before the basic commercial question is known.

Operate, scale or stop

When a venture produces useful evidence, it can earn more attention and investment. When it does not, it can change, wait or end without threatening the parent House.

A capability is not a customer. A green test is not a sale. Evidence decides what earns the next pound and the next hour.
House of Carol working principle

Shared standards

Different ventures. One standard of judgement.

The customer proposition may change, but the House expects the same basic disciplines wherever it operates.

1

Truth before claims

State what is known, what is being tested and what has not yet been demonstrated.

2

Smallest competent intervention

Use the minimum complexity, technology, cost and structure necessary to produce a reliable result.

3

Commercial reality matters

Attention, compliments and internal activity are not substitutes for willingness to pay, useful outcomes and sustainable economics.

4

Learning changes the next decision

Useful evidence should improve the venture, the House method or the decision to stop.
