Fictional worked example

A clearer route from trade-account application to first order

A simple illustration of what becomes visible when one customer journey is reviewed from beginning to end instead of step by step.

Illustrative only — not a real customer, case study or claimed result.

Alderwick Trade Supplies · fictional trade-supply business

The situation

Every individual step made some sense. Together, they were hard to follow.

Alderwick’s trade-account journey had grown over time. A prospective customer found the account information online, downloaded an application, returned it separately, waited for review, received a decision and then went through another activation and account-setup process before ordering.

The issue was not that every control was wrong. It was that the customer had to piece together several connected activities as though they were separate processes.

Illustrative current journey
Find account information
Download application
Return it separately
Wait for review
Receive decision
Activate online
Complete account setup
Ready to order

What the review looks for

Friction, hand-offs and controls in the same picture.

Channel changes

The journey moved between web content, a separate application route, communications and online activation. Each change added another place for context to be lost.

Disconnected stages

Application, decision and activation were closely connected for the customer but behaved like separate internal processes.

Waiting without enough visibility

Once the application entered review, the customer had limited information about status, further requirements or what would happen next.

Requirements discovered late

Additional users, delivery locations and purchasing controls could have been identified earlier and routed to the right person.

Controls that still matter

Credit decisions, authorisations and higher-risk account changes still need accountable judgement. Making the journey clearer does not mean removing necessary control.

A clearer target journey

Make one connected service feel like one connected service.

A target journey could organise the experience around a single understandable route while leaving credit decisions, approvals and other necessary controls with the business.

Illustrative target journey
Understand the account
Apply through one route
Receive confirmation and reference
Application reviewed
Provide anything further
Receive decision
Activate securely
Ready to order
