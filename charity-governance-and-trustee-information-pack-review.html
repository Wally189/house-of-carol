What changes

Trustee information becomes easier to use for real decisions: the ask, evidence, risks, assumptions and actions are made clearer and more proportionate.

The review improves information quality without replacing trustee judgement, legal advice or regulator authority.

What you will receive

Pack review

A structured review of the agreed trustee pack against its purpose, decisions, evidence, risks and actions.

Decision clarity

Identification of the exact decision, recommendation or discussion required, with ambiguity and missing authority made visible.

Evidence/risk critique

A challenge of material evidence, assumptions, uncertainties and risks without replacing trustee legal advice or judgement.

Actionability

A check that agreed actions, owners, dependencies and follow-up are clear enough to be carried into governance practice.

Improvement recommendations

Prioritised changes to make the pack shorter, clearer and more decision-useful while preserving necessary evidence and accountability.
