Website Release Assurance

A second pair of eyes before your customers find the problems.

Independent pre-release QA for businesses, agencies and organisations approaching a website launch, redesign, campaign release or significant update.

£149Fixed launch price. Up to 10 agreed public pages/templates, one primary user journey and one retest of up to 10 reported fixes.

Ask Sales about a review

What we check

The customer-facing release, not just the code.

  • desktop and mobile/responsive behaviour in the agreed and available environments;
  • navigation, links, buttons and one agreed form or conversion journey where safely testable;
  • visual hierarchy, layout, spacing, typography and obvious consistency defects;
  • first-time-user usability and friction;
  • content presentation, obvious copy defects and inconsistent or unsupported claims;
  • basic accessibility preflight including keyboard/focus, resize/reflow, enlarged text and obvious labelling/structure issues where testable;
  • release-oriented SEO basics including titles, headings, internal links and accidental indexing/noindex signals;
  • non-intrusive public-surface security/privacy sanity checks for obvious release mistakes.

What you receive

A report you can act on.

  • a prioritised issue register with location, evidence, severity, why it matters and recommended action;
  • findings separated between observation, reproduced fact, judgement and uncertainty;
  • a concise executive recommendation: RELEASE, RELEASE WITH ACCEPTED EXCEPTIONS or HOLD;
  • one retest of up to 10 reported fixes submitted within 7 calendar days.

Turnaround

Target: 2 working days.

The target starts when the agreed URL, scope and any genuinely necessary safe test access are available. If the release is larger than the fixed package, we say so before work starts rather than quietly stretching the job.

Not included

A release review is not every kind of assurance.

The £149 service does not include penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, formal WCAG certification, exhaustive browser/device-lab testing, load testing, legal certification, development/design remediation or unlimited ongoing support. Anything materially different is discussed and quoted separately before work proceeds.

Access and privacy

Public-site-only by default.

The launch service is designed to avoid unnecessary customer data and passwords. If staging credentials or personal data become genuinely necessary, the minimum access and handling arrangement is agreed separately before that element begins. Passwords should not be sent in ordinary email.

Payment is by bank transfer using the private details supplied on the invoice.
