Shared Drive & Document Cleanup

If “where is the latest version?” is a normal question, the system needs sorting.

Shared drives rarely become a mess all at once.

A new folder appears because somebody cannot find the old one. Another copy is made “just in case”. Projects finish but never quite get archived. Nobody wants to delete anything because nobody is certain what is still needed.

Eventually the organisation has plenty of documents and very little confidence about where the right one lives.

House of Carol takes one shared document area, works out what is actually going on, and gives you a simpler structure and a controlled plan for putting it right.

For: Owners, operations managers, office/business managers, charity operations leads and team leads who are responsible for a shared document area but no longer trust the structure.

£1,250 fixed fee

One team or business function · one shared document area.

Nothing is deleted or moved by House of Carol under the standard review without explicit customer approval.

What changes

  • people regularly ask where documents live;
  • several copies appear to be “the latest”;
  • old and current work are mixed together;
  • folder structures have grown around individual habits;
  • onboarding includes a long explanation of where things are hidden;
  • nobody is quite sure who owns the structure;
  • previous tidy-ups created another folder rather than solving the problem.

What you will receive

A current-state map showing the main areas and problems.

Clear source-of-truth and ownership decisions.

A simpler target structure.

Practical naming and version rules.

A keep / move / archive / review plan.

A retrieval map showing where common information should live.

A step-by-step implementation checklist.

A handover session to make sure the plan works for the people who have to use it.

What needs to be in place?

A named owner for the document area.

A short discovery session.

Safe read-only access where appropriate, or a sufficient folder/file inventory, screenshots or directory listing.

Any existing rules the organisation already follows.

We prefer the minimum access needed to understand the problem. We do not need to read every document simply because it exists.

What is outside the scope?

We do not arrive and start deleting folders.

Where something is stale, duplicated or unclear, we classify it and show the decision that needs making.

Nothing is deleted or moved by House of Carol under the standard review without explicit customer approval.

If the problem is actually organisation-wide, involves multiple systems or requires a bulk migration, we scope that separately rather than pretending it fits the fixed review.

Is this the right service?

If everyone can reliably find the current version, ownership is clear and the problem is mostly that you dislike the folder names, keep your £1,250.

If you simply need somebody to move thousands of files, this standard review is not a bulk migration service.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No open-ended consulting meter.

No automatic deletions.

No invented retention rules.

No attempt to turn one messy team drive into an enterprise information-governance programme.
