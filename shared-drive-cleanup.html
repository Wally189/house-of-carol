Shared Drive & Document Cleanup

If “where is the latest version?” is a normal question, the system needs sorting.

Shared drives rarely become a mess all at once. A new folder appears because somebody cannot find the old one. Another copy is made “just in case”. Projects finish but never quite get archived.

House of Carol takes one shared document area, works out what is actually going on, and gives you a simpler structure and a controlled plan for putting it right.

When it is useful

Plenty of documents. Very little confidence about where the right one lives.

People regularly ask where documents live.

Several copies appear to be “the latest”.

Old and current work are mixed together.

Folder structures have grown around individual habits.

Onboarding includes a long explanation of where things are hidden.

Previous tidy-ups created another folder rather than solving the problem.

What you get

A practical route from “messy drive” to a controlled structure.

  • a current-state map showing the main areas and problems;
  • clear source-of-truth and ownership decisions;
  • a simpler target structure;
  • practical naming and version rules;
  • a keep / move / archive / review plan;
  • a retrieval map showing where common information should live;
  • a step-by-step implementation checklist;
  • a handover session to make sure the plan works for the people who have to use it.

How it works

Understand first. Move nothing by accident.

01

Define

Agree the team, shared area, accountable owner and the problems that actually matter.

02

Map

Review the current structure and enough representative evidence to understand how it is really being used.

03

Sort

Identify source-of-truth, duplicate, stale, ownership and retrieval problems without assuming every duplicate is wrong.

04

Design

Create the smallest sensible target structure, naming rules, archive approach and retrieval map.

05

Plan

Give you an ordered, non-destructive cleanup and implementation checklist with clear approval points.

Not a mass delete

We do not arrive and start deleting folders.

Where something is stale, duplicated or unclear, we classify it and show the decision that needs making.

Nothing is deleted or moved by House of Carol under the standard review without explicit customer approval.

The standard service is a review, target structure and controlled cleanup/migration plan. Bulk migration or organisation-wide reorganisation is a different piece of work.

The standard review

One team. One shared area. One primary platform.

Normally returned within 7 working days once the agreed access or inventory and discovery inputs are complete.

If the problem is organisation-wide, spans several systems or requires bulk migration, we scope that separately rather than pretending it fits the fixed review.

The standard service is designed around mainstream shared-document environments such as Google Drive, Microsoft 365 / SharePoint / OneDrive or an equivalent repository where safe review evidence can be provided.

What we need

Enough access to understand the problem. No more than that.

A named owner for the document area and a short discovery session.

Safe read-only access where appropriate, or a sufficient folder/file inventory, screenshots or directory listing.

Any existing rules the organisation already follows, plus the problem areas you already know about.

We prefer the minimum access needed to understand the problem. We do not need to read every document simply because it exists.

Who it is for

People responsible for a shared document area they no longer trust.

Likely fit

  • owners and managing directors;
  • operations managers and COOs;
  • office and business managers;
  • charity operations leads;
  • governance or administration leads;
  • team leads responsible for shared information.

Probably not worth buying

If everyone can reliably find the current version, ownership is clear and the problem is mostly that you dislike the folder names, keep your £1,250.

If you simply need somebody to move thousands of files, this standard review is not a bulk migration service.
