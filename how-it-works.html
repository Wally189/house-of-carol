How it works

Understand first. Decide what matters. Finish something useful.

House of Carol keeps the problem, evidence, scope and result visible from the first conversation to the final handover.

Bring us a problemExplore defined offers

The working sequence

A bounded job from problem to usable result.

01

Establish the real problem

Start with the outcome, the evidence, the people involved and what is actually getting in the way.

02

Agree the job

Make the inputs, outputs, responsibilities, timing and boundaries understandable before work begins.

03

Do the work carefully

Use appropriate methods and technology while keeping judgement and accountability with a named person.

04

Show what we found

Connect important conclusions to their evidence and keep uncertainty or missing information visible.

05

Leave a usable result

Return something clear enough to decide from, work from or improve, with the next actions identified.

What you can expect

Clear scope. Clear evidence. Clear next steps.

Facts and assumptions stay separate. Unsupported certainty is not useful, so material gaps and dependencies remain visible.

Technology serves the work. Digital tools and AI may help where appropriate, but they do not replace human judgement or become the product you are expected to buy.

Boundaries are part of the result. We state what was examined, what was not, and where a specialist or regulated professional is required.

A poor fit can be a valid answer. If the House is not the right fit, or a simpler route would do, saying so is useful work.

Two ways to start

Choose an offer, or describe the job.

The catalogue contains defined, fixed-fee offers with their own scope, deliverables, timing and exclusions.

If the work does not fit a service name, describe what you are trying to achieve, what is getting in the way and what a useful result would look like. The first task is to understand the job, not force it into the wrong box.
