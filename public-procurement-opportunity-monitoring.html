Public Procurement Opportunity Monitoring

Provide defined, filtered monitoring of public procurement opportunities against an agreed customer profile.

For: SMEs and VCSEs seeking public contracts.

It fits an SME or VCSE that repeatedly needs relevant public procurement opportunities filtered against a stable profile.
