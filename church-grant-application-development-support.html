Service

Church Grant Application Development Support

For: Parishes and church organisations

Support the parish to organise and present a truthful, coherent funding application efficiently.

The problem

What this service is for.

Applications require evidence, narrative, budgets and coordination that stretch limited parish capacity.

What you get

A defined piece of useful work.

  • Application plan
  • evidence assembly
  • draft support
  • timetable
  • review
  • submission-readiness checklist

Delivery

How the work is framed.

Service type
Professional service
Delivery
Remote
What we need from you
Applicant ownership; accurate project/budget evidence; funder rules.

Boundaries

What is not included.

Parish remains applicant and decision-maker; no fabricated claims; no funding guarantee.

Pricing

Project fee

Exact price is confirmed before you commit, once the scope, timing and responsibilities are clear.
