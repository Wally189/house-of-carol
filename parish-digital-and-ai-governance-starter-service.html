Parish Digital & AI Governance Starter Service

Provide practical, proportionate digital/AI governance suited to parish operations and volunteer contexts.

Staff and volunteers may use digital and AI tools without sufficiently clear data, safeguarding or use rules.

For: Parishes and diocesan teams.

£1,500 standard — indicative scope band £1,000–£2,250. Final price is confirmed after scope fit.

A defined professional service centred on tool/use review, data/ai rules, acceptable-use guidance and the remaining listed deliverables. The exact boundary is confirmed before commitment.

Temporal service only · competent Church authority remains with the customer · no fabricated funding or ecclesiastical claims

Discuss the project

What changes

Staff and volunteers get clearer day-to-day rules for using the agreed digital and AI tools, with visible ownership and escalation routes.

The service turns competent local decisions into practical operating guidance; it does not claim diocesan, canonical, legal, DPO or safeguarding authority.

What you will receive

Tool/use review

A concise inventory of the digital and AI tools, account types and parish use cases inside the agreed scope.

Data/AI rules

Practical rules that translate the parish’s approved privacy, safeguarding and organisational requirements into everyday use decisions.

Acceptable-use guidance

Clear staff and volunteer guidance on permitted use, prohibited use, checking responsibilities and when to ask for help.

SOP

A bounded operating procedure for the agreed digital/AI activity, including ownership, approval and escalation points.

Briefing

A practical briefing that explains the rules through parish-relevant examples without pretending to replace specialist advice.

Escalation points

A clear route for safeguarding, privacy, security, canonical, legal or other questions that sit outside the standard service.

What needs to be in place?

Current parish tools.

Safeguarding/data context.

Competent local authority.

Only the minimum information and access needed for the agreed work should be provided. Passwords, MFA codes, private keys and unnecessary confidential or personal data are not requested through ordinary messages.

What is outside the scope?

No canonical/legal opinion beyond competence.

No diocesan policy authority.

Anything materially outside the agreed boundary becomes a separate decision rather than hidden expansion of the engagement.

Is this the right service?

The organisation fits the intended customer: Parishes and diocesan teams.

There is an accountable customer owner able to provide the information and decisions the work depends on.

If the main need falls outside the stated deliverables or requires a specialist judgement House of Carol is not competent or authorised to provide, a different route is required.

Customer decisions and competent professional, legal, regulatory or ecclesiastical authority remain with the appropriate owner.
