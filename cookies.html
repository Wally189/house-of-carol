Cookies & storage

No tracking theatre.

House of Carol keeps the website deliberately simple. The current site contains no House-added analytics, advertising tags or preference-storage scripts.

Current position

House of Carol does not currently add analytics cookies, advertising cookies, marketing pixels, fingerprinting scripts or local-storage preferences to this website. There is therefore no House cookie-consent banner.

The site is hosted by a third-party web host, which may process ordinary technical request information needed to serve and secure the site. That hosting activity is covered in the privacy notice.

If this changes

If House of Carol later introduces a technology that stores or accesses information on a visitor’s device, the use will be reviewed before release. Where UK rules require consent, the technology will not be activated before valid consent is obtained.

A cookie banner will not be added merely because websites usually have one. It will be added only if the technology actually requires it.

Official guidance

The Information Commissioner’s Office explains the UK rules for cookies and other storage/access technologies. Non-essential technologies normally need clear information and appropriate consent.
