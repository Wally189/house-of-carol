Cookies & storage

No tracking theatre.

The current House of Carol preview contains no House-added analytics, advertising tags, marketing pixels or preference-storage scripts.

Current position

House of Carol does not currently add analytics cookies, advertising cookies, marketing pixels, fingerprinting scripts or local-storage preferences to this website. There is therefore no House cookie-consent banner.

The current primary pages are static and do not deliberately store or submit visitor choices to House of Carol.

The site is hosted by a third-party web host, which may process ordinary technical request information needed to serve and secure the site. That hosting activity is addressed in the privacy notice.

If this changes

If House of Carol later introduces analytics or another technology that stores or accesses information on a visitor’s device, the use will be reviewed before release. Where UK rules require consent, the technology will not be activated before the appropriate consent route is in place.

A cookie banner will not be added merely because websites usually have one. It will be added only if the actual technology makes it necessary.

Official guidance

The Information Commissioner’s Office explains the UK rules for cookies and other storage/access technologies. Non-essential technologies normally need clear information and appropriate consent.
