Scope
One accountable management owner, 5–8 KPI definitions, up to five relevant existing source systems/files and one lightweight reporting pack.

The standard scope is management information, not statutory accounts, bookkeeping, audit or tax advice.

What needs to be in place?

One accountable management owner.

Clearly stated management decisions.

Up to five relevant existing source systems or files.

Customer-approved definitions where finance/accounting measures are material.

What is outside the scope?

No statutory accounts, bookkeeping, audit, tax or regulated advice.

No fabricated targets or benchmarks.

No enterprise data warehouse or complex BI integration.

No individual employee productivity surveillance by default.

No guaranteed business outcome.

Is this the right service?

It fits an SME that needs a small, decision-useful management-information baseline from data it already has.

If the main need is statutory financial reporting, a complex data platform or workforce surveillance, this is not the standard service.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No KPI, dashboard or report is represented as a guarantee of business performance or as statutory accounting assurance.
