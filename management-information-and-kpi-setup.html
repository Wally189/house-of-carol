Management Information & KPI Setup

The need

What problem does this address?

Management lacks decision-useful performance information or relies on vanity metrics.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Define a small set of useful measures and a practical reporting structure tied to real decisions.

The work is delivered as a bounded advisory / implementation. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Management decision/information map
  • 5–8 KPI definitions/dictionary
  • data/source map
  • one lightweight management reporting pack/template
  • reporting rhythm
  • ownership
  • review/action triggers
  • handover

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Confirm prerequisites, customer-owned access, data boundaries and acceptance criteria.
  2. Design and implement only the authorised bounded scope in the customer’s environment or agreed artefact set.
  3. Test the result, including fallback, recovery or exception handling where relevant.
  4. Complete quality, privacy and security checks that the exact facts trigger.
  5. Run customer acceptance and hand over the operating guidance, ownership and bounded remedy route.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One management-information setup using up to five existing source systems/files and 5–8 justified KPIs.

What we need from you

  • One accountable management owner
  • clearly stated management decisions
  • up to five relevant existing source systems/files
  • customer-approved definitions where finance/accounting measures are material
  • source ownership/data-quality visibility

Timing

No standard turnaround commitment is currently authoritative; timing is confirmed after complete inputs and scope fit.

Boundaries

What is outside scope or not being promised?

Outside the standard scope

  • No statutory accounts/bookkeeping/audit/tax or regulated advice
  • no fabricated targets/benchmarks
  • no enterprise data warehouse or complex BI integration
  • no individual employee productivity surveillance by default
  • no guaranteed business outcome

No unsupported outcome claim

No guarantee of a particular business, funding, compliance, procurement, learning, revenue, productivity or other outcome is created beyond the exact bounded product record and evidence available.

Any wider or materially different work requires explicit re-scoping and may trigger additional specialist assurance.
