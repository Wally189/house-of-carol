What changes

The reporting starts with the decisions management actually needs to make, then defines the smallest useful set of measures, sources, owners and review triggers.

Measures are included because they support a real management decision, review or action — not simply because they are easy to count.

What you will receive

Management decision/information map

Connect the reporting need to real management decisions.

5–8 KPI definitions

A practical KPI dictionary covering definition, calculation or logic, unit, source, owner, frequency, relevant data-quality limitations and review/action trigger.

Data/source map

Show where each measure comes from, who owns the source and where important data-quality limitations remain.

Lightweight reporting pack

One practical management reporting pack or template using suitable existing customer tools, or a platform-neutral specification where direct setup is not appropriate.

Reporting rhythm and ownership

Define who updates, reviews and acts on the information.

Review/action triggers

Make the point of measurement explicit by linking information to action.

Handover

Remote handover plus one bounded correction cycle for genuine in-scope factual, formula, source-mapping or agreed-scope defects.

Scope
One accountable management owner, 5–8 decision-linked KPIs, up to five relevant existing source systems/files, one lightweight reporting pack/template or platform-neutral specification, a defined reporting rhythm, remote handover and one bounded correction cycle.

The standard scope is management-information design and bounded setup. It does not include statutory accounts, bookkeeping, payroll, audit, tax advice, regulated financial advice or assurance over the customer’s underlying records or data. Finance or accounting measures may be included where the customer supplies or approves the relevant definitions and source basis.

What needs to be in place?

One accountable management owner who can approve the decisions, definitions and reporting approach.

Clearly stated recurring management decisions or questions that the reporting needs to support.

Up to five relevant existing source systems or files, with source ownership, available descriptions and known data-quality limitations made clear.

Customer-approved definitions where finance or accounting measures are material, together with timely information and approvals needed for the agreed scope.

What is outside the scope?

No statutory accounts, bookkeeping, payroll, audit, tax advice, regulated financial advice or assurance over underlying records or data.

No complex BI implementation, enterprise data warehouse, API integration, automated ETL or replacement of accounting, CRM or ERP systems.

Targets, benchmarks or thresholds are only treated as established where there is a sound customer-approved or evidenced basis for them.

No individual employee productivity surveillance as a standard use of the service.

No guaranteed business outcome, forecast accuracy or underlying data quality.

Is this the right service?

It fits an organisation established in England and Wales buying for business purposes, or one coherent management team or function within one organisation, that already has information but needs a small, decision-linked management-information baseline.

If the main need is statutory financial reporting, more than five source systems or files, more than eight standard KPIs, complex BI or systems integration, or workforce surveillance, this is not the standard service.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No KPI, dashboard or report is represented as a guarantee of business performance, forecast accuracy, underlying data quality or statutory accounting assurance.
