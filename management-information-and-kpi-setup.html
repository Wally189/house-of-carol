What changes

House of Carol helps you choose a small set of useful measures, define them properly and put them into a practical reporting structure using the information and tools you already have.

What you will receive

What you will get

A focused set of 5–8 management measures linked to the decisions you need to make.

These are often called key performance indicators, or KPIs. In plain English, they are the few numbers you use to understand what is happening, spot something that needs attention and decide what to do next.

The £1,250 service includes:

a clear map of the management questions and decisions the reporting needs to support;

5–8 agreed management measures;

a clear definition for each measure, including how it is calculated;

review of up to five existing systems, spreadsheets, reports or other information sources;

a record of where each measure comes from and any important problems or limitations in the underlying information;

one lightweight management reporting pack or template using suitable tools you already have, or a written specification where direct setup is not appropriate;

agreement on who updates and reviews the information, how often it should be reviewed and when a result should prompt attention or action;

remote handover; and

one correction round for errors in the agreed work.

How it works

Start with the decisions

We begin with the recurring questions management needs to answer.

That keeps the work focused on information that helps someone make a decision or take action rather than numbers that are simply easy to count.

Check the information you already have

We review up to five existing systems or files.

We identify where the relevant information comes from, who is responsible for it and whether there are important gaps, inconsistencies or reliability problems.

We do not hide uncertainty in the underlying information.

Define the measures

We agree 5–8 useful measures and document each one clearly.

For each measure we record:

  • what it tells you;
  • how it is calculated;
  • where the information comes from;
  • who is responsible for it;
  • how often it should be reviewed; and
  • when the result should prompt attention or action.

House of Carol does not invent targets, benchmarks, accounting treatments or source information.

Put it into a usable reporting format

We create one lightweight reporting pack or template using suitable tools you already have.

Where direct setup is not appropriate, we provide a clear written specification instead.

The aim is a reporting structure that can be understood, maintained and used in normal management discussions.

Finance and accounting

This is a management-information service, not an accounting or financial-advice service.

It does not provide statutory accounts, bookkeeping, audit, tax calculation or advice, regulated financial advice, or verification of accounting records.

Measures involving finance or accounting can be included where you provide or approve the definition and the information they are based on.

What is outside the scope?

What is not included

The £1,250 service does not include:

  • more than five existing systems or information sources;
  • more than 8 management measures;
  • reporting covering additional teams or business functions beyond the agreed piece of work;
  • complex business-intelligence systems or data warehouses;
  • automation or systems integration;
  • individual employee productivity surveillance;
  • guaranteed business results, forecast accuracy or guarantees about the quality of information supplied to us; or
  • work involving sensitive personal data or specialist regulated work that requires separate assessment.

Anything outside this service is not added to the £1,250 price without being discussed and agreed separately.
