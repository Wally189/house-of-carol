Management Reporting Setup

Make the numbers you already have useful for management decisions.

Turn existing spreadsheets, systems and reports into a small set of clearly defined measures tied to real management questions.

For: small UK organisations or management teams that already have spreadsheets, systems or reports but still cannot answer a few recurring management questions reliably.

£1,250fixed

Up to five existing systems or files. We agree 5–8 management measures and document them clearly.

Remote delivery. The timetable is confirmed once the agreed source material and management questions are available.

No invented targets or benchmarks. We work from your real sources and make important limitations visible.

The problem

When reporting gives you numbers but not answers

You can collect plenty of information and still struggle to tell what needs attention. Measures may exist because they are easy to count, definitions may differ between reports, figures may arrive too late, or nobody may be sure which source is authoritative.

The result is more reporting without more clarity.

The outcome

What changes

We start with the recurring management questions you need to answer, then work backwards to the information and measures that genuinely support those decisions.

You finish with a smaller, clearly defined set of measures, known sources and owners, and a practical reporting structure with a review rhythm and action triggers.

The work

What you will receive

Decision and information map

The recurring management questions that matter and the information needed to answer them.

KPI set and dictionary

5–8 management measures, each with a clear purpose, definition, calculation logic, source, owner, review frequency, limitations and review or action trigger.

Source review and data map

Review of up to five existing systems or files, showing where the information comes from and any material quality caveats.

Reporting pack or specification

One lightweight management reporting pack or template using suitable tools you already have, or a clear written specification where direct setup is not appropriate.

Rhythm, ownership and triggers

A practical arrangement for who maintains the information, how often it is reviewed and what should trigger attention or action.

Handover and correction

Remote handover plus one correction round for errors in the agreed work.

The process

How it works

1. Start with the decisions

We agree the recurring questions management needs the reporting to answer.

2. Check what you already have

We review up to five existing systems or files, identify useful information, source ownership and material gaps or reliability issues.

3. Define the measures

We agree 5–8 useful measures and document them consistently. House of Carol does not invent targets, benchmarks, accounting treatments or source information.

4. Put them into a usable reporting structure

We create one lightweight reporting pack or template, or a clear written specification where direct setup is not appropriate, then complete a remote handover.

Your part

What we need from you

One accountable person must be able to confirm the management questions, agree definitions and provide the relevant existing information and source ownership.

  • Up to five existing systems or files containing the relevant information.
  • Known data-quality issues and source limitations.
  • Definitions or accounting treatments that your organisation already uses where a measure involves finance.
  • Timely factual clarification where sources conflict or a measure cannot be established reliably.

Fit and boundaries

Where this fits — and where it does not

A good fit

You already have useful information in existing systems or files but need a clearer set of decision-useful measures, definitions, ownership and reporting rhythm.

Probably not needed

If your important measures are already consistently defined, reliably sourced and routinely used to make the decisions you need to make, this service is unlikely to add enough value.

Outside the standard service

Complex BI implementation, APIs or ETL, more than five sources, more than eight KPIs, ongoing managed reporting, statutory accounts, bookkeeping, audit, tax work, regulated financial advice, individual employee productivity surveillance and specialist regulated work.
