AI Workflow Implementation Sprint

Turn one proven AI use case into a working, controlled process your team can actually use.

If you already know where AI could help but the work still depends on ad-hoc prompting, individual know-how or inconsistent checking, this sprint turns one validated use case into one repeatable human-operated workflow inside your existing ChatGPT Business or Enterprise workspace.

For: Small organisations in England and Wales with one defined, recurring, low- or moderate-consequence AI use case and an existing ChatGPT Business or Enterprise workspace.

£1,500 fixed fee

The standard sprint covers one organisation, one team or function, one recurring workflow, one existing customer-owned supported workspace and one ChatGPT Project OR one custom GPT.

Planned delivery: normally within seven working days once the agreed information, access route and scope are complete.

Human-operated · tested against normal and failure cases · manual fallback retained · no integration or unattended automation hidden inside the scope

Discuss the workflow

What changes

You finish with a defined working method rather than a loose collection of prompts.

The workflow and its purpose are explicit; approved inputs and sources are defined; one Project or custom GPT is configured; staff know what the AI may and may not be used for; important outputs have human checks and approval points; normal and failure cases are tested; a manual fallback remains available; and the method is documented for handover.

The sprint does not promise a percentage time saving, accuracy rate, revenue gain or return on investment. Any benefit has to be observed in real use rather than invented in advance.

What you will receive

Implementation brief

A concise definition of the agreed workflow, operator, business purpose, inputs, outputs, source-of-truth information, constraints, information classes, human authority and stop conditions.

Current state and fallback

The existing way of doing the work is captured before change, together with the manual fallback or rollback route that remains available if the AI workflow is unavailable or unsuitable.

One configured ChatGPT workflow

One customer-owned ChatGPT Project OR one custom GPT configured inside the existing Business or Enterprise workspace using the agreed instructions and only the knowledge, files and capabilities permitted by the customer’s current settings.

Input, source and human-control rules

Practical rules for what can be supplied, what must not be supplied, source priority, verification, human approval, exceptions and stop conditions.

Test pack and results

Up to ten representative agreed cases, proportionate to the workflow, covering normal use plus relevant boundary and failure conditions. Results show what passed, failed, was corrected, remains limited or remains unknown.

SOP and quick-use card

Reproducible operating instructions covering the steps, checks, stop conditions, known limitations, fallback and escalation route.

Handover

A 60-minute remote walkthrough with the workflow owner or operator, including a demonstration, questions and the known limitations and responsibilities.

Bounded defect support

Ten working days after handover and acceptance for defects in the agreed configuration, instructions or SOP. New features, additional workflows, redesign after a provider change and ongoing monitoring are new scope.

Before anything is agreed, House of Carol confirms that the workflow, workspace, information, access route and human controls fit this standard unit. If they do not, the work is paused, narrowed, declined or routed rather than stretched beyond the service.

What needs to be in place?

The use case should already have been validated as worth testing, through an AI Workflow Opportunity Review or equivalent evidence, and the underlying process needs to be stable enough to implement.

There must be one accountable workflow owner and at least one intended operator, with the existing/manual method available as fallback.

The customer must already own and control a ChatGPT Business or Enterprise workspace with the native permissions needed for the agreed Project or custom GPT.

The business purpose, approved information, source-of-truth material and final human approval point must be clear. Any necessary privacy, confidentiality or security conditions must be resolved before representative live-data testing.

How is human control retained?

A person intentionally starts each run and selects or supplies the input. AI output is something to review, verify, revise or reject rather than an automatic decision.

Source-dependent claims are checked through the agreed source route before reliance. Missing information, uncertainty and conflicting evidence are not silently filled. A competent customer human retains final authority before operational or external use.

The standard workflow does not autonomously send messages, approve decisions, publish content, submit forms, change a system of record or make consequential decisions.

What is outside the scope?

The standard sprint does not include Apps or GPT Actions calling third parties, APIs, webhooks, MCP or custom connectors, Zapier, Make, Power Automate, custom application development, scheduled or unattended execution, automatic external sending or write-back, multiple connected systems, a second workflow, new paid software, model fine-tuning, bespoke retrieval infrastructure, public GPT publication or ongoing managed support.

It does not provide legal advice, DPO services, DPIA sign-off, cybersecurity audit, penetration testing, compliance certification or a regulated-sector professional opinion.

The standard first-test unit does not cover clinical or patient-care decisions, safeguarding decisions, employment selection or dismissal, credit or eligibility decisions or equivalent high-consequence decision systems.

No customer password, MFA code, recovery code, API key or other secret is required.

How is this different from automation implementation?

This sprint is for one human-operated workflow inside one supported ChatGPT workspace. A person starts the work, supplies the input, checks the result and keeps final authority.

If the intended outcome depends on event triggers, APIs or webhooks, system-to-system data movement, scheduled or unattended steps, automatic routing or write-back, the requirement belongs to the separate No-Code / AI Automation Implementation service rather than this sprint.

What are the limitations of the AI?

Generative AI can produce incorrect, incomplete or unsupported output and its behaviour can change. This sprint does not pretend those limitations disappear.

The implementation instead makes appropriate inputs, verification, human authority, known limitations, failure behaviour, fallback and change testing explicit for the one agreed workflow.

Is this the right service for our organisation?

It is designed for an organisation that has already selected a real recurring workflow for AI implementation, understands the current process, already uses ChatGPT Business or Enterprise and wants a consistent working method while keeping a person responsible for starting, checking and approving the work.

A different route may be better if the real problem is still deciding where AI is useful, selecting a vendor, designing organisation-wide AI governance, repairing a broken process or automating activity across systems.
