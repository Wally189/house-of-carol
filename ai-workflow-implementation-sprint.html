AI Workflow Implementation Sprint

Convert one validated use case into one tested, repeatable, human-operated AI workflow inside the customer’s existing supported AI workspace, with clear instructions, approved inputs/sources, human-control points, acceptance tests, manual fallback, SOP and handover.

For: Small England-and-Wales B2B service organisations with one validated low/moderate-consequence AI use case and an existing customer-owned ChatGPT Business or Enterprise workspace.

The need

What problem does this address?

A promising AI use case has not been converted into a reliable working process that staff can operate with clear inputs, controls, tests, fallback and ownership.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Convert one validated use case into one tested, repeatable, human-operated AI workflow inside the customer’s existing supported AI workspace, with clear instructions, approved inputs/sources, human-control points, acceptance tests, manual fallback, SOP and handover.

The work is delivered as a bounded implementation service. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Implementation brief
  • AS-FOUND baseline/fallback
  • target workflow map
  • one configured ChatGPT Project OR custom GPT
  • input/source rules
  • human-control points
  • up to 10 representative test cases and results
  • SOP/quick-use card
  • 60-minute handover
  • acceptance/offboarding record
  • bounded defect remedy

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Confirm prerequisites, customer-owned access, data boundaries and acceptance criteria.
  2. Design and implement only the authorised bounded scope in the customer’s environment or agreed artefact set.
  3. Test the result, including fallback, recovery or exception handling where relevant.
  4. Complete quality, privacy and security checks that the exact facts trigger.
  5. Run customer acceptance and hand over the operating guidance, ownership and bounded remedy route.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One validated use case; one customer-owned supported ChatGPT workspace; one configured Project or custom GPT; up to 10 representative test cases.

What we need from you

  • One validated use case
  • one stable workflow
  • accountable workflow owner/operator
  • existing ChatGPT Business/Enterprise workspace
  • customer-approved purpose/data/access
  • manual fallback
  • security/privacy clearance where triggered
  • no integration or new-spend requirement

Timing

Timing is confirmed after complete inputs and scope fit; no unsupported SLA is implied.

Boundaries

What is outside scope or not being promised?

Outside the standard scope

  • No Apps/Actions/APIs/webhooks/MCP/custom connectors/custom code
  • no Zapier/Make/Power Automate
  • no scheduled/unattended execution or automated external send/write-back
  • no second workflow/team
  • no new paid tooling
  • no public GPT
  • no high-consequence regulated decision use
  • no legal/DPIA/cyber certification
  • no guaranteed savings/ROI/accuracy/compliance

No unsupported outcome claim

No guarantee of a particular business, funding, compliance, procurement, learning, revenue, productivity or other outcome is created beyond the exact bounded product record and evidence available.

Any wider or materially different work requires explicit re-scoping and may trigger additional specialist assurance.

Specialist boundary

AI, data and technology boundary

The service does not create legal, data-protection, cybersecurity or compliance certification. Exact tool, data, access and high-consequence facts can trigger Legal, Privacy or Security review and a STOP before work proceeds.
