Discuss the service

What changes

The customer gains a repeatable editorial route from approved facts and priorities to a coherent newsletter or stakeholder update that is ready for its approval.

House of Carol produces the agreed content; the customer retains factual ownership, voice and publication authority.

What you will receive

Editorial plan

An agreed editorial direction linking audience, purpose, priority topics and customer-owned facts for the in-scope edition or service unit.

Researched copy

Draft copy grounded in the customer’s approved information and any agreed external sources, with unsupported claims removed or qualified.

Production

Preparation of the agreed edition or content package in the defined format, without silently expanding into a wider publishing service.

Approval workflow

A clear draft-review-approval route that leaves final publication authority with the customer.

Edition schedule

An agreed schedule for the defined edition or recurring unit once cadence, capacity and turnaround have been explicitly set.
