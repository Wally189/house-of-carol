The need

What problem does this address?

Organisations lack capacity for consistent, useful newsletters or stakeholder updates.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Provide a repeatable editorial and production service grounded in customer-approved facts and priorities.

The work is delivered as a bounded managed content service. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Editorial plan
  • researched copy
  • production
  • approval workflow
  • edition schedule

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Agree the service profile, eligible requests or sources, finite capacity and intake route.
  2. Complete each agreed cycle or request within the bounded service unit.
  3. Quality-check sources, outputs and material exceptions before delivery.
  4. Record consumption, unresolved issues, overage or specialist referrals.
  5. Review the service periodically and use change control before any wider scope or renewal.
  6. Review facts, claims, copyright, accessibility and specialist approval triggers.
