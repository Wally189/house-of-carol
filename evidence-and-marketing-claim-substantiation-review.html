Evidence & Marketing Claim Substantiation Review

Check whether your customer-facing claims are stronger than the evidence behind them.

For SMEs and charities preparing factual, comparative or performance claims and wanting a clear evidence check before release.

Outcome: a claim-by-claim view of what is supportable, what needs qualification or correction, what needs specialist referral, and what should not be used on the evidence available.

£1,000 standard

Indicative scope band: £750–£1,500.

One intended customer-facing use or version, up to 15 material claims and up to 20 material evidence or source items.

Remote. Normally planned around 5 working days once the claim set is frozen and the evidence bundle is complete.

Discuss the review

When this service is useful

A claim does not need to be obviously false to create a problem. It may rely on a weak source, omit an important qualification, use a comparison with no clear basis, or turn an aspiration into a statement of fact.

This review is useful when you want to challenge the wording before release rather than discover afterwards that the evidence does not support what was said.

What changes

You move from a page, proposal or campaign containing untested assertions to a structured record of each material claim, the evidence behind it and the action needed before release.

The review does not create proof that is not there. It makes the evidence position, qualifications, gaps and residual limits visible so your organisation can make its own publication decision.

What you receive

  • Claim and evidence register: the material claims in scope matched to the evidence and source items supplied for them.
  • Substantiation status: each claim returned as supported, supported with qualification, specialist referral required, or insufficient — do not use.
  • Qualification and correction recommendations: the smallest changes needed where the evidence does not support the wording as written.
  • Residual-limit note: a clear record of material evidence gaps, unresolved questions and matters that the review cannot establish.
  • One bounded factual correction cycle: one correction round for factual or review-misreading issues within the agreed scope.

How it works

  1. Fix the boundary. Agree the intended customer-facing use or version and freeze the material claim set for review.
  2. Supply the evidence. Provide the underlying evidence and source material you want relied on for those claims.
  3. Test each claim. We examine the wording against the evidence and identify the appropriate status, qualification or referral.
  4. Receive the findings. You receive the register, statuses, qualifications, correction recommendations and residual limits.
  5. Correct factual misreadings. One bounded factual correction cycle is included before the review closes.

What we need from you

  • The customer-facing use or version being reviewed.
  • A clear, frozen set of material claims.
  • The underlying evidence and source material you want the review to rely on.
  • Your own approval for final wording, publication and any specialist sign-off required.

The five-working-day planning basis applies only once the agreed claim set is frozen and the evidence bundle is complete.

Is this a good fit?

This service is designed for an SME or charity preparing factual, comparative, performance or other evidence-dependent customer-facing claims and wanting an evidence-honest challenge before release.

It is a good fit where you can identify the exact claim set, the intended customer-facing use and the evidence you want relied on.

What this service does not do

This is not legal advertising clearance, regulator approval, formal audit or certification, a legal opinion, a guarantee of regulator acceptance, or a way to invent missing evidence.

Legal, regulatory, compliance, security, accessibility and certification claims may need referral to the competent specialist before you decide whether or how to publish them.

No claim is certified commercially effective. Your organisation retains authority for final wording, publication and any legal or specialist sign-off required for its circumstances.
