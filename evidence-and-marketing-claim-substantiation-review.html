Evidence & Marketing Claim Substantiation Review

Challenge objective customer-facing claims against the evidence before release.

Customer-facing claims may exceed available evidence or omit material qualifications.

For: SMEs and charities publishing factual or performance claims.

Review fee — the claim classes, evidence threshold, review unit, turnaround and fee are confirmed before commitment.

A bounded claim review covering the claim inventory, underlying evidence/source register, substantiation status, limitations/qualifications, correction recommendations and residual-limit note.

Claim-by-claim evidence challenge · limitations visible · not legal advertising clearance or a guarantee of regulator acceptance

Discuss the review

What changes

Claims are separated from the evidence that supports them so overstatement, missing qualification and unsupported certainty can be corrected before release.

The exact review unit and threshold depend on the claim classes and are not invented on this page.

What you will receive

Claim inventory

A clear list of the material claims in scope.

Evidence test

Check the source/evidence actually offered for each claim.

Substantiation status

A visible status showing where evidence supports, partly supports or does not support the wording.

Qualifications

Material limitations and qualifications needed for evidence-honest wording.

Correction recommendations

Prioritised corrections where the evidence does not support the claim as written.

Scope
A bounded inventory/evidence/substantiation review for the agreed claim classes.

The current product does not pretend that one evidence threshold already covers factual, comparative, quantified-performance, testimonial, environmental/ethical and forecast claims alike.

What needs to be in place?

Access to the underlying evidence.

A clear intended claim and intended use.

What is outside the scope?

Not legal advertising clearance.

No guarantee of regulator acceptance.

Specialist legal judgement remains separately triggered where required.

Is this the right service?

It fits an organisation that has objective customer-facing claims and wants an evidence challenge before release rather than a rubber stamp.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No claim is certified lawful, regulator-approved or commercially effective. The service tests evidence and wording within the agreed boundary.
