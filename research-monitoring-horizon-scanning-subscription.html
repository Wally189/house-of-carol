What changes

The customer gets a defined watch rather than an unbounded promise to “monitor everything”: meaningful changes are turned into sourced alerts and implications within an agreed source and capacity boundary.

Coverage limits, cadence and alert thresholds are explicit; the service does not guarantee that every external development will be captured.

What you will receive

Defined watch

A bounded monitoring definition covering the topic, source universe, material-change threshold and intended audience.

Periodic monitoring

Repeat review of the agreed sources at the explicitly agreed cadence and within the finite capacity boundary.

Sourced alerts

Concise alerts when a change meets the agreed threshold, with direct provenance rather than unsourced headlines.

Implications brief

A short explanation of why the change may matter, what remains uncertain and whether specialist interpretation is required.

Change log

A running record of material alerts, source changes and monitoring decisions so the service remains auditable and maintainable.
