AI Adoption Support Retainer

Keep AI adoption moving without turning every new question into another project.

Once an organisation has started using AI in real work, questions do not stop at handover. New use cases appear, tools change, staff need decisions and an existing rule or workflow may need a small adjustment.

This retainer gives existing House of Carol AI customers a defined route for that continuing work, with a visible monthly boundary and a clear distinction between support and a new implementation project.

For: existing House of Carol AI customers that already have a usable baseline and need proportionate ongoing adoption support.

A finite monthly support service. The agreed support level sets the included capacity and response arrangements before the retainer begins.

Monthly boundary: support is agreed within a maximum of up to four bounded requests, one monthly review of up to three agenda items and six hours in the month.

What changes

Recurring questions get a clear home instead of being handled through ad-hoc messages and one-off favours.

You have a defined route for new use questions, practical adoption issues and small agreed updates.

Monthly priorities are reviewed against the existing AI baseline rather than starting again each time.

Questions, decisions, actions and referrals remain visible, so unresolved matters do not disappear into informal conversations.

When a request is actually a new implementation, specialist matter or materially larger piece of work, it is identified and scoped separately rather than silently absorbed into the retainer.

What you will receive

A bounded support route around the AI work already in place.

Monthly review

One recurring review of the agreed AI adoption position, with up to three agenda items within the selected support level.

Controlled support requests

A clear route for bounded practical questions and requests, prioritised within the agreed monthly capacity.

New-use assessment

Proportionate review of proposed new AI uses so the organisation can decide whether to test, hold, change route or stop.

Small policy or process updates

Bounded amendments to existing AI material where they fit the agreed monthly support unit.

Action and decision log

A visible record of material questions, decisions, actions, unresolved points and referrals arising through the service.

Clear escalation

Early identification of work that needs a separate implementation, specialist input or a larger engagement, with the reason made clear before further work is agreed.

How the monthly boundary works

The retainer is continuing support, not unlimited advisory access.

The exact monthly support level is agreed before the service begins. Across the service, the operating ceiling is up to four bounded support requests, one monthly review with up to three agenda items and no more than six hours of House of Carol time in a month.

Unused capacity does not roll into a later month. A materially new implementation, larger redesign or specialist matter is separated out and only proceeds if separately agreed.

What needs to be in place?

An existing House of Carol AI engagement or agreed baseline, one accountable customer contact and a controlled route for submitting support requests.

The organisation remains responsible for its operational decisions, system permissions, data handling and any specialist approvals that apply to its work.

What is outside the scope?

The retainer is not unlimited on-demand advisory access and does not include an unlimited number of requests or hours.

A new implementation project, materially larger redesign, regulated professional opinion or specialist legal, privacy, security or other expert work is not silently absorbed into monthly support.

Is this the right service?

It is a good fit when useful AI work is already in place and the organisation needs a finite route for recurring questions, small updates and new-use decisions.

If the need is a one-off policy, training session, opportunity review or implementation project, one of the defined project services is likely to fit better.

What does House of Carol not promise?

The retainer does not promise unlimited availability, instant responses, a particular level of AI adoption, compliance outcomes, workload, savings, productivity gains or commercial results.
