What changes

Questions and new use cases move through a controlled support route rather than informal, unbounded advice.

The service unit, capacity, response arrangements, carry-over, overage and pause/termination rules are not invented on this page; they must be agreed before commitment.

What you will receive

Periodic review

A defined recurring review of the agreed adoption position.

Q&A and controlled intake

A bounded route for practical questions and requests.

New-use assessment

Proportionate assessment of new proposed AI uses.

Policy/process updates

Bounded updates where the retainer scope expressly includes them.

Performance support

Practical adoption support within the agreed service unit.

Action log

A visible record of questions, decisions, actions and referrals.

What is outside the scope?

No unlimited advisory access.

Specialist matters remain referred.

New implementation projects or materially larger work are not silently absorbed into the retainer.

Is this the right service?

It is intended for existing AI customers that need a finite, governed route for ongoing adoption questions and change.

If the organisation needs a one-off policy, review, training or implementation project, another service is likely to fit better.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No unlimited availability, instant response, adoption outcome, compliance result or guaranteed workload is implied.
