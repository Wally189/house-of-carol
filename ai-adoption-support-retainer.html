AI Adoption Support Retainer

Give recurring AI questions and small changes a reliable place to go.

Useful AI work keeps moving after the first project. Staff raise new questions, tools change, a new use case needs checking and an existing rule or workflow may need a small update.

This service gives existing House of Carol AI customers a defined support route around the baseline already in place, without treating every question as a new project.

What changes

A continuing route for the practical questions that appear once AI is part of real work.

What you will receive

Baseline review

Periodic review of the AI baseline already in place and the material adoption questions that have emerged around it.

Controlled support questions

A defined route for practical questions that can be answered within the existing service boundary and House of Carol competence.

New-use assessment

Proportionate assessment of a proposed new AI use so the organisation can decide whether it fits the existing baseline, needs further work or should not proceed yet.

Bounded updates

Small agreed changes to existing AI policy, process or guidance material where the change does not amount to a new implementation project.

Adoption support

Practical help where staff or managers need to apply the existing rules and working methods more consistently.

Action, change and referral record

A clear record of material questions, agreed changes, unresolved items and matters that need a separate specialist or implementation route.

The service is finite. The agreed monthly scope is confirmed before work starts so both sides know what support is included and when a request has become a separate piece of work.

What needs to be in place?

An existing House of Carol AI engagement or agreed baseline, one accountable customer contact and an agreed route for support requests.

The organisation remains responsible for its operational decisions, system permissions, data handling and any specialist approvals that apply to its work.

What is outside the scope?

The retainer is not unlimited or emergency/on-call advisory access.

New implementation projects, automation builds, materially larger redesigns, legal or DPO work, certification and other specialist professional matters are not included unless separately scoped and agreed.

Is this the right service?

It fits when useful House of Carol AI work is already in place and the organisation wants a defined route for recurring questions, small updates and new-use decisions.

If the need is a one-off policy, training session, opportunity review or implementation project, a defined project service is likely to fit better.

What does House of Carol not promise?

The retainer does not promise unlimited availability, instant responses, a particular level of AI adoption, compliance outcomes, savings, productivity gains or commercial results.
