The need

What problem does this address?

Organisations need continuing support as tools, use cases and guidance change.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Provide ongoing governed support for new use cases, questions, updates and practical adoption issues.

The work is delivered as a bounded managed service. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Periodic review
  • Q&A
  • new-use assessment
  • policy/process updates
  • performance support
  • action log

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Agree the service profile, eligible requests or sources, finite capacity and intake route.
  2. Complete each agreed cycle or request within the bounded service unit.
  3. Quality-check sources, outputs and material exceptions before delivery.
  4. Record consumption, unresolved issues, overage or specialist referrals.
  5. Review the service periodically and use change control before any wider scope or renewal.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One managed-service customer per month with an accepted House of Carol AI baseline. The unit is capped at up to four bounded support requests plus one monthly review of no more than three agenda items and a maximum six active service hours per month, whichever request or time ceiling is reached first.

What we need from you

  • Accepted House of Carol AI baseline
  • named customer owner
  • one controlled intake
  • customer participation within the agreed bounded unit
  • triggered specialist routes where required

Timing

Monthly bounded service unit; response timing is agreed within that unit and is not represented as an SLA.

Boundaries

What is outside scope or not being promised?

Outside the standard scope

  • No unlimited advisory access
  • specialist matters remain referred

No unsupported outcome claim

No guarantee of a particular business, funding, compliance, procurement, learning, revenue, productivity or other outcome is created beyond the exact bounded product record and evidence available.

There is no carry-over. Specialist substantive work is excluded after disposition or handoff; overage or new scope is held, separately scoped or deferred.

Recurring-service boundary

A finite recurring unit is explicit.

One managed-service customer per month; up to four bounded support requests plus one monthly review of no more than three agenda items; maximum six active service hours per month; whichever request or time ceiling is reached first. Unused capacity expires and there is no carry-over. Emergency/on-call work and work beyond competence STOP.
