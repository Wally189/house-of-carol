What needs to be in place?

A defined learner audience.

A current source basis and the employer's relevant policy context.

An agreed delivery platform and sponsor able to answer contextual questions.

Only the minimum information needed for tailoring should be provided; unnecessary sensitive data is not required.

What is outside the scope?

No accredited qualification or regulated competence claim unless separately authorised.

The session does not replace role-specific professional training, organisational policy or specialist legal, privacy or security advice.

Is this the right service?

It is for employers that need an adult workplace baseline in safe, responsible and effective AI use rather than generic awareness theatre.

If the need is role-specific workflow practice, leadership governance, policy implementation or regulated specialist competence, another route is likely to fit better.

What does House of Carol not promise?

Attendance does not prove workplace competence, behavioural change or productivity improvement. No certification, compliance or business-outcome guarantee is implied.
