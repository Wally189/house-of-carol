Why this exists

Polished is not the same as reliable.

An invented statistic sounds ordinary enough that nobody thinks to challenge it.

A source looks authoritative until somebody checks the date, context or actual wording.

A conclusion is written confidently even though the evidence only partly supports it.

The same team has read the draft so many times that everyone is now too close to see the problem.

Another AI gives a confident critique — but you still do not know whether the critique itself is right.

The output is about to go to a client, board, funder or public audience and “it looks fine” is not enough.

What we test

Evidence, claims and reasoning — not just grammar.

  • unsupported or suspicious factual claims;
  • statistics without a reliable source;
  • stale or weak evidence;
  • source and claim not actually matching;
  • false certainty and overclaiming;
  • reasoning gaps and hidden assumptions;
  • internal contradictions;
  • important counterarguments the draft ignores;
  • conclusions that go further than the evidence;
  • important uncertainty hidden by confident wording.

This is not a normal proofread. The question is not merely whether the document reads well. The question is where it could fail when somebody relies on it.

Defect severity

Know what matters first.

BLOCKER / MAJOR

  • Blocker: do not use the output in the stated way until this is corrected or referred.
  • Major: could materially change the reliability, conclusion or decision.

MODERATE / MINOR

  • Moderate: a meaningful weakness that should be addressed.
  • Minor: lower-impact clarity, consistency or presentation weakness.

Why not just ask another AI?

Because another confident answer is still another confident answer.

Another AI can help

Sometimes asking another model for criticism is perfectly sensible and may be all you need.

But another model can repeat the same weakness, invent a different answer or produce a critique that sounds rigorous without being well evidenced.

What House of Carol adds

An accountable human reviewer owns the final judgement. AI may help generate challenge questions where the data route allows it, but a material finding is not treated as established merely because another model said it.

Important findings are tied back to evidence or explicit uncertainty.

Not a fit

Not legal advice or legal sign-off, clinical or medical judgement, safety-critical assurance, regulated financial advice, statutory audit/certification, penetration testing or a full AI-system/model evaluation.

If you need somebody to guarantee that an output is “safe”, “legally compliant” or “error-free”, this is not that service.

If the consequence is trivial and a competent colleague can challenge it properly, £750 is probably too much. Use the colleague.
