The House rule

Do not build the theatre before the evidence.

—

No speculative catalogue

Ideas still being researched remain inside the House until there is enough evidence to explain them truthfully.

—

No tool-first answers

Software, automation and AI are means, not automatic solutions. They have to improve the result.

—

No dependency by design

Where work is handed over, the customer should be able to use the result without needing us permanently in the room.
