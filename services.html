Current services

Two problems we can define clearly.

House of Carol is testing two bounded B2B services. One improves an existing recurring workflow. The other independently checks a website before or around release. Neither is an open-ended consultancy engagement.

01

Workflow implementation

Use it when: an existing administrative or operational process is repeatedly slow, duplicated, confusing, error-prone, overly manual or dependent on awkward hand-offs.

What we do: capture the current route, identify avoidable friction, design the smallest practical improvement, implement the agreed bounded change where feasible, test the normal route and material exception, then hand it back.

What you receive: current-state findings, an improved workflow design, the implemented change where agreed, test evidence, operating notes and known limitations.

02

Website release assurance

Use it when: a new website, redesign, campaign page or material update is approaching launch or handover and you want somebody independent to check the important path.

What we do: inspect up to the agreed pages/templates and primary journey across the agreed environments, checking navigation, responsive behaviour, links and buttons, usability, accessibility preflight, content presentation, release SEO basics and observable public-surface privacy/security issues.

What you receive: a prioritised issue register, evidence for material findings, a RELEASE / RELEASE WITH ACCEPTED EXCEPTIONS / HOLD recommendation and one bounded retest of reported fixes within the agreed window.

What we will not pretend

Boundaries are part of the product.

—

No invented results

House of Carol does not yet claim external customer outcomes, guaranteed savings, conversion uplifts or a defect-free result.

—

No automatic technology prescription

A new app, automation or AI system is not assumed to be the answer. Existing tools and simpler process changes come first where they are sufficient.

—

No false breadth

These services do not include large software builds, regulated professional advice, penetration testing or formal accessibility certification unless a future, separately authorised proposition says otherwise.
