What £495 can cover.

The exact pieces depend on the real workflow, but the work stays inside one commercial path from accepted or completed work through invoicing and payment visibility.

Typical repair territory

  • clear accepted, delivered and ready-to-invoice states;
  • named ownership and hand-off triggers;
  • minimum customer, quote and job information carried forward once;
  • simple status visibility and exception handling;
  • customer messages at useful points in the journey;
  • invoice-preparation templates or checklists where needed;
  • sensible reminder cadence using existing tools;
  • lightweight forms, trackers or low-risk automation where justified;
  • manual fallback and recovery notes.

Not included

  • bookkeeping or accounting;
  • tax or financial advice;
  • debt collection or legal recovery;
  • credit decisions;
  • holding customer funds;
  • acting as an outsourced finance department;
  • open-ended transformation work;
  • regulated professional work outside House competence.

How the engagement works.

The process is deliberately small enough to understand and controlled enough to hand back cleanly.

01 · FIT

Agree the boundary.

Start, end, desired outcome, existing systems, dependencies and exclusions.

02 · TRACE

Follow the real work.

Observe people, decisions, waits, retyping, status and exceptions rather than guessing from the software list.

03 · BUILD

Implement the repair.

Remove and simplify first; build only the low-risk pieces that improve the agreed workflow.

04 · TEST & HAND BACK

Leave it usable.

Test ordinary and awkward cases, document what matters and return control to the customer.

Why this is different

The invoice may be fine. The workflow around it may not be.

House of Carol is not selling invoicing software. We look at the operating state that tells somebody work is billable, who owns the next action, where the information comes from and how exceptions become visible.

Simple principle

Design the hand-off before automating it.

Automation can accelerate a good path. It can also make a badly defined path fail faster and more invisibly.
