A good fit

You recognise the symptom, even if you cannot name the cause.

The Rescue is intentionally small. It is not a whole-company review. It is for one repeatable piece of work that creates disproportionate chasing, duplication, memory or catch-up.

Enquiries

Leads or requests need too much chasing.

Information arrives in several places, follow-up depends on memory or the customer cannot see what happens next.

Onboarding

Every new person or customer starts a scavenger hunt.

Documents, tasks, checks and responsibilities live across inboxes, files and people’s heads.

Approvals

Nobody is quite sure who has the current version.

Work sits between people while approval, evidence or ownership is reconstructed.

Reporting

The same figures are rebuilt every week or month.

Copying, checking and reformatting have become the process instead of the reporting.

What happens afterwards

The Rescue does not trap you into another purchase.

You own the answer and can implement it yourself, with your team or with another competent supplier. Sometimes that is exactly what should happen.

If you want House of Carol to help put an agreed low-risk change in place, we can separately scope The Fix.

Optional · The Fix

Build the practical change, then hand it back.

Where it fits our competence, The Fix can cover the working artefacts around the redesigned process — for example templates, trackers, forms, SOPs, file/document structures, hand-off packs or simple workflows.

  • separate written scope and price;
  • existing tools first where sensible;
  • testing and recovery before consequential change;
  • customer approval and control; and
  • usable documentation at handover.

There is no public fixed price for The Fix yet. Scope and effort vary, and we will not invent a price merely to make the website look complete.
