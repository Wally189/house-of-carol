What the Fix can touch

The working path
around the invoice.

The invoice itself may be fine. The friction often sits in what tells somebody to raise it, where the information comes from, how status is seen and what happens when the normal path fails.

TYPICAL REPAIR TERRITORY

Use the tools already there before buying another one.

  • a clear accepted / delivered / ready-to-invoice state;
  • named ownership and hand-off triggers;
  • minimum quote, customer and job information carried forward once;
  • simple status visibility and exception handling;
  • customer messages at the useful points in the journey;
  • invoice-preparation templates or checklists where genuinely needed;
  • sensible reminder cadence using existing accounting/payment tools where appropriate;
  • lightweight forms, trackers or low-risk automation only when they beat the simpler option;
  • manual fallback and recovery route; and
  • editable handover with the customer in control.

How it works

Four moves. No consultancy maze.

The exact artefacts depend on the business. The operating logic does not.

01 · Fit

Bound the real problem.

Where does the commercial path start and end? What is actually broken? What existing system is authoritative? Is this within House competence?

02 · Trace

Follow the As Found work.

People, systems, decisions, waits, retyping, customer messages, status and exceptions are followed end to end rather than guessed from the software list.

03 · Build

Make the Better State work.

Remove, simplify and standardise first. Build only the agreed low-risk artefacts or configuration and test ordinary and awkward cases.

04 · Hand back

The customer owns it.

Clear operating notes, editable material, recovery information where relevant and no artificial House dependency.

BOUNDARY

What this is not

Not bookkeeping. Not debt collection. Not financial advice.

House of Carol fixes the operating workflow around commercial administration. It does not make accounting entries, advise on tax, decide credit, hold customer money, pursue debts, send legal demands or act as the customer’s outsourced finance department. Accountants, providers and legal professionals keep their proper jobs.

See how the House controls scope

Fit rules

The £495 should buy a real boundary.

If the problem cannot be bounded honestly, the House should not squeeze it into a fixed price merely to make a sale.

RULE 01

One commercial workflow, not the whole company.

The starting wedge is the path from agreed/accepted work through billable status, invoice and payment visibility. A materially unrelated operations problem does not get smuggled into the same scope.

RULE 02

No guaranteed saving or payment acceleration.

The House can remove avoidable delay, duplication and chasing from the process. It cannot control whether or when a customer pays and will not manufacture an ROI number.

RULE 03

Existing tools get the first audition.

A new app, integration or AI feature must beat the simpler option after setup effort, cost, lock-in, privacy, recovery and the customer’s real operating capability are considered.

What happens after?

Nothing automatically.

The Fix stands on its own. If the work reveals another valuable workflow and the customer wants help with it, House of Carol may scope a separate piece of work.

No retainer is hiding behind the curtain.
