When it earns its keep

The warning signs are ordinary.

The rescue is deliberately narrower than a consultancy programme. It is for one repeatable process that wastes attention, creates chasing or depends too heavily on somebody remembering what happens next.

01

Information gets copied

The same details are retyped between emails, spreadsheets, documents or systems.

02

People keep chasing

Work stalls until somebody asks where something is, who has it or what is still missing.

03

One person holds it together

The process works because somebody remembers the exceptions, not because the process is clear.

04

A new tool did not fix it

Software changed, but the old steps, duplicated records or unclear ownership stayed behind.

You give us one defined process. We diagnose that process. If the problem is actually larger, regulated or outside scope, we say so rather than quietly turning a small job into a programme.

WHAT COMES BACK

Enough to act. Nothing added for bulk.

  • an As-Found map of what really happens;
  • the main breakpoints, delays, duplication and weak hand-offs;
  • immediate low/no-cost fixes;
  • a simpler Better-State design;
  • technology or automation judgement only where it earns its place;
  • a visual action summary in the format that best fits the process; and
  • one clarification round.

The rescue stands on its own. You can implement the recommendations yourself or discuss further work separately.
