The need

What problem does this address?

Funding opportunities are fragmented and time-consuming to identify and assess.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Provide a current, prioritised view of credible funding routes matched to the temporal project need.

The work is delivered as a bounded research service. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Funder research
  • eligibility screen
  • deadlines
  • evidence requirements
  • priority list
  • source links

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Define the question, opportunity or funding/tender requirement and verify scope or eligibility.
  2. Gather current authoritative evidence and record sources, assumptions and limitations.
  3. Analyse the evidence against the declared criteria without fabricating missing facts.
  4. Quality-check citations, claims and material qualifications.
  5. Complete factual review and hand over the decision-ready output; submission or external contact remains separately authorised.
  6. Use authoritative Church sources first where doctrine, morals, ecclesial authority or Catholic publication boundaries are engaged.
  7. Keep House of Carol’s temporal service distinct from pastoral, sacramental, canonical or teaching authority.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One defined church/parish funding research brief, matched to the stated temporal project need.

What we need from you

  • Clear project need
  • current funder sources
  • parish authority to use information

Timing

Timing is confirmed after complete inputs and scope fit; no unsupported SLA is implied.

Boundaries

What is outside scope or not being promised?

Outside the standard scope

  • No guarantee of funding
  • no sale of spiritual goods or ecclesiastical authority

No unsupported outcome claim

No outcome guarantee is implied. Product-specific exclusions and evidence limits remain controlling.

Any wider or materially different work requires explicit re-scoping and may trigger additional specialist assurance.

Specialist boundary

Church authority boundary

This is a temporal professional service. Competent Church officeholders retain pastoral, sacramental, canonical and ecclesiastical authority. Where the exact work triggers Canon Law, diocesan policy or competent ecclesiastical approval, House of Carol stops and routes that requirement before proceeding.
