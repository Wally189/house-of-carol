Church Grant Funding Research

Find the grants worth your parish’s time.

Grant funding is fragmented. The useful question is not “what grants exist?” but “which live routes genuinely fit this project, what do they require, and what should we do next?”

House of Carol researches one defined church or parish project and returns a practical funding decision brief.

£595 fixed

One organisation · one defined project · England · normally within five working days once the agreed information is complete.

Current primary-source research · eligibility and fit screened · no funding guarantee, success fee or ecclesiastical authority claim

Discuss the funding need

What you get

Funding-readiness snapshot

A concise view of the project information, evidence and permissions already in place and what may still be needed.

Prioritised shortlist

Credible opportunities screened for eligibility and fit, with the strongest routes shown first.

Grant sizes and deadlines

Grant amount or range, deadlines and match-funding requirements where the source publishes them.

Evidence and permissions

Material evidence, permission or support requirements that affect whether a route is worth pursuing.

Near-misses and exclusions

Useful routes that do not fit, with the reason, rather than padding the shortlist.

Action order and source trail

A practical next-action order and evidence checklist, with direct source links and the date checked.

The shortlist is not padded. If only a small number of grants genuinely fit, that is what the brief will say.

What the brief looks like

Each shortlisted route is presented as a decision entry:

Fit: why it may fit your project.

Amount: grant amount or range, where published.

Deadline: the current published deadline.

Match funding: the requirement, where published.

Evidence or permission: what is needed before the route can progress.

Next action and source: recommended next step and the primary source checked.

The brief then puts the routes in priority order so clergy, staff or trustees can see what is worth investigating first.

Why trust the research method

House of Carol uses current primary funder sources wherever reasonably available, records when information was checked, distinguishes confirmed facts from unknowns, and does not pad the shortlist.

If something important is unknown, we say so rather than inventing an answer. The brief includes direct source links and the date checked so your parish can verify the basis for the conclusions itself.

What we need from you

Project purpose, location, likely cost if known, timing, relevant building or listed status, condition or quinquennial information if available, known church or diocesan permission or support status, and any funders already considered.

What is not included?
  • writing or submitting grant applications;
  • contacting funders or church authorities for you;
  • guaranteeing eligibility or funding success;
  • building, conservation, legal or canonical advice;
  • securing diocesan or other ecclesiastical permissions;
  • running a capital campaign; or
  • ongoing funding monitoring after the agreed research date.

Church and diocesan decisions remain with the competent church authority. Our fee is for professional research only; it does not purchase or promise any spiritual, pastoral, sacramental, canonical or ecclesiastical outcome.

When is it probably not worth buying?

Keep your £595 if:

  • you do not yet have a defined project or need;
  • a competent person has already completed an up-to-date funding search for the same project;
  • you only want someone to submit an application; or
  • the relevant funding route is already known and you simply need bid-writing support.
What does the price cover?

£595 fixed. No success fee. No percentage of any grant award. No guarantee that a grant will be available or awarded.
