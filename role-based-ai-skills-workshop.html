The need

What problem does this address?

Generic AI training does not translate into better performance in specific roles.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Teach and test practical AI use on recurring workflows of one defined role/team, requiring source verification, safe data/tool judgement, human authority and independent transfer rather than generic prompting.

The work is delivered as a bounded training service. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • 60-minute role/workflow diagnostic
  • three mapped workflows
  • three-hour live remote workshop for up to 12 adults
  • three workflow labs
  • independent transfer case
  • work-method cards
  • application rubric
  • manager debrief

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Confirm the sponsor, audience, objectives, prerequisites and participant boundary.
  2. Tailor the approved content to the stated work context without widening the service.
  3. Quality-check materials, claims and accessibility before delivery.
  4. Deliver the session and test practical understanding or application.
  5. Provide the agreed outputs, correct material defects and close with clear next actions.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One coherent role family or team; up to 12 adults; three representative workflows; one three-hour live remote workshop.

What we need from you

  • One coherent role family
  • three representative workflows
  • approved tools/data/customer policy context
  • source-of-truth/approval routes
  • customer participation

Timing

A three-hour live remote workshop after the role/workflow diagnostic and agreed scheduling.
