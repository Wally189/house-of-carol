The need

What problem does this address?

The business cannot clearly explain how essential work would continue through common disruption.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Create a proportionate continuity baseline around critical services, dependencies, fallback and recovery.

The work is delivered as a bounded advisory / design. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Critical-service and impact-over-time map
  • tolerable disruption/target recovery
  • minimum viable operation
  • dependency and light disruption-risk analysis
  • continuity/fallback procedures
  • activation/communications and recovery ownership
  • technology-recovery assumptions/referrals
  • exercise plan
  • review/maintenance triggers
  • action/referral list
  • bounded factual correction cycle

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Confirm the governing requirements, current process, users, roles and decision rights.
  2. Design the bounded policy, process or SOP artefacts around the actual operating need.
  3. Route legal, privacy, security or other specialist questions when the facts trigger them.
  4. Quality-check the draft and complete a controlled customer review and revision cycle.
  5. Hand over the approved artefacts with ownership, version and maintenance rules.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One small organisation; one accountable continuity owner; up to 5 critical services/functions and 10 material dependencies.

What we need from you

  • Accurate/current service and dependency information
  • one accountable continuity owner
  • customer-owned risk appetite, tolerable outage, recovery targets/priorities and activation decisions
  • timely factual review
  • specialist facts disclosed on trigger

Timing

Timing is confirmed after complete inputs and scope fit; no unsupported SLA is implied.

Boundaries

What is outside scope or not being promised?

Outside the standard scope

  • Not ISO 22301 certification/full BCMS, formal audit/conformity assessment, emergency-service/statutory emergency planning, live-incident management, specialist safety/security or cyber assurance, penetration testing, technical disaster-recovery engineering/testing, exercise execution, business-interruption insurance advice or guarantee of uninterrupted operations/recovery times

No unsupported outcome claim

No guarantee of a particular business, funding, compliance, procurement, learning, revenue, productivity or other outcome is created beyond the exact bounded product record and evidence available.

Any wider or materially different work requires explicit re-scoping and may trigger additional specialist assurance.
