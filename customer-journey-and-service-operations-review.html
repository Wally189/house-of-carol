The need

What problem does this address?

Customer experience is fragmented across teams, channels or processes.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Map the end-to-end customer journey, identify operational friction and service failures, and show where the journey could become clearer and more effective without removing necessary business controls.

The work is delivered as a bounded advisory service. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Current-state journey map
  • friction and service-failure analysis
  • evidence gaps
  • priority improvements
  • bounded target-state journey where warranted by the evidence and agreed scope

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Confirm the decision, scope and evidence inputs before review begins.
  2. Review the supplied material against declared criteria and current authoritative sources.
  3. Record assumptions, limitations, control gaps and matters that require specialist referral.
  4. Quality-check the findings and discuss factual corrections with the customer.
  5. Hand over the findings, recommendations and agreed next actions.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One end-to-end customer journey for one defined service line/customer segment, with up to three channels and six material roles/handoffs.

What we need from you

  • One accountable service owner
  • defined customer segment and journey start/end
  • accurate current process/service evidence
  • known mandatory controls
  • customer participation
  • up to three channels and six material roles/handoffs

Timing

Timing is confirmed after complete inputs and scope fit.

Boundaries

What is outside scope or not being promised?

Outside the standard scope

  • No invented customer sentiment
  • no primary customer research, implementation, CRM/website build, full UX redesign or broad transformation in standard scope
  • no removal of necessary controls
  • no guaranteed NPS/retention/conversion/revenue improvement

No unsupported outcome claim

No guarantee of a particular business, funding, compliance, procurement, learning, revenue, productivity or other outcome is created beyond the exact bounded product record and evidence available.

Any wider or materially different work requires explicit re-scoping and may trigger additional specialist assurance.
