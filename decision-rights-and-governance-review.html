Decision Rights & Governance Review

Make it clearer who decides what — and where a decision goes when it cannot be resolved

If routine decisions keep escalating, several groups discuss the same issue, or nobody is quite sure who has authority to decide, the problem may be the decision structure rather than the decision itself.

For: SMEs and charities with an accountable sponsor who can explain how decisions are meant to work — and how they work in practice.

What changes

House of Carol maps how important decisions currently move through your organisation, identifies duplication and unclear ownership, and shows where decision and escalation routes could be made clearer.

What you will receive

Current decision map

A clear map of the material decision routes in scope and how they operate now.

Decision and ownership map

A practical view of which decisions are currently reserved, delegated or escalated, together with the roles responsible for them.

Escalation and referral routes

Clear routes for exceptions, conflicts and matters that cannot be resolved at the normal level.

Governance-routine review

A review of the meetings and governance routines in scope, including duplication, unnecessary hand-offs and gaps in ownership.

Prioritised recommendations

A focused set of changes addressing the most important sources of ambiguity, duplication or delay.

Proposed future decision route, where justified

Where the evidence supports a change, a proportionate target route showing clearer ownership, escalation and governance arrangements.

One factual and scope correction cycle

An opportunity to correct factual misunderstandings or agreed-scope errors before final handover.

How the review works

1. Agree the boundary

We confirm the decisions, roles and governance routines that are in scope and the information needed to review them.

2. Map the current reality

We trace how the selected decisions are meant to work and how they actually move through the organisation.

3. Identify friction and ambiguity

We look for duplicated routes, unclear ownership, unnecessary escalation, gaps and differences between formal authority and established practice.

4. Develop proportionate recommendations

Where change is justified, we set out a clearer decision, ownership and escalation route without adding unnecessary governance.

5. Review and hand over

The work is quality-checked, you review it for factual accuracy, and the agreed outputs are handed over.

What needs to be in place?

What we need from you

The review needs one accountable organisational sponsor and access to enough current information to understand the decision routes properly.

That will normally include relevant governance or decision documents, examples of the recurring decisions in scope, the roles involved and the regular meetings or routines through which those decisions currently pass.

We will also need someone who can explain where formal arrangements differ from day-to-day practice and review the findings for factual accuracy.

What is outside the scope?

This review is not the right service if

You need a legal opinion, company-law or charity-law advice, formal constitutional drafting, a determination of statutory powers, or formal legal or regulatory sign-off.

It is also not a substitute for a company secretary, board secretary, governance officer, legal adviser or other specialist officeholder.

Where a decision-rights question depends on legal or constitutional interpretation, that part of the matter is referred for competent specialist advice rather than decided by House of Carol.

Your organisation remains the decision-maker

House of Carol can map existing arrangements, distinguish documented authority from established practice and recommend a clearer route.

We do not transfer, exercise or determine your organisation's formal decision-making authority. Your organisation remains responsible for its governance decisions and for obtaining any legal, constitutional or regulatory approval that is required.

Is this the right service?

This review is likely to fit if

You have recurring decisions that:

  • move between several people or meetings without a clear owner;
  • escalate more often than they appear to need to;
  • are discussed in several places before anyone decides;
  • rely on informal practice that does not clearly match the documented arrangement; or
  • repeatedly produce uncertainty about who should decide or what happens next.
What does House of Carol not promise?

What we do not promise

The review does not guarantee faster decisions, financial savings, legal compliance or a particular organisational outcome.

Its purpose is narrower: to give you a clear, evidence-based view of the decision routes in scope and practical recommendations for making ownership and escalation easier to understand.
