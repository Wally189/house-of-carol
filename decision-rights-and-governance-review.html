What needs to be in place?

Access to current governance and decision processes.

What is outside the scope?

Does not replace company or charity legal advice.

Does not provide formal constitutional drafting or legal determination.

Is this the right service?

It fits an SME or charity where decisions are slow, duplicated, unclear or made through shadow routes and leadership wants a proportionate operating fix.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No legal validity, statutory power or governance outcome is certified. The review supports clearer operating decisions within the customer's actual authority.
