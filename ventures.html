Ventures

Only operating or sufficiently developed ventures belong here.

House of Carol keeps a wider internal pipeline, but this corporate site does not publish speculative ideas as though they were finished businesses.

Current position

The specialist business-services venture is being prepared separately.

House of Carol is developing a specialist customer-facing business for practical operational, administrative, digital and professional work. Its role is to let customers meet a focused specialist proposition rather than make the corporate House site behave like a consultancy catalogue.

The specialist site is not publicly released yet. Until it is approved, House of Carol remains the corporate provenance layer and this page remains deliberately restrained.

What will appear here later

Once a venture is operating publicly, this page can provide a concise description and link to its own customer-facing home. Ventures that do not need a public website do not receive one merely for symmetry.

Public when earned.

A venture appears here when its proposition and status can be described truthfully.

Specialist when useful.

Distinct buyers can be served through focused brands without pretending they are separate legal companies.

No catalogue theatre.

Internal ideas, investments and experiments stay internal until there is a reason for the public to care.
