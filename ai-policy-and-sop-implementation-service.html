What do you need from us?

A named decision owner, a clear policy perimeter, the AI tools and use contexts that matter, relevant existing policies or client restrictions, and access to the people who own privacy, security, HR or IT decisions where those issues are triggered.

We do not need passwords, MFA codes, private keys or unnecessary raw sensitive records.

How does it work?

1. Confirm

Confirm the problem, scope and decision owner.

2. Understand

Understand the AI tools and use contexts the policy needs to govern.

3. Map requirements

Map the relevant legal, regulator, security and customer-specific requirements without pretending guidance is legislation.

4. Draft

Draft the policy, staff guide, role matrix and selected SOPs.

5. Challenge

Challenge the pack against realistic normal and failure cases.

6. Hand over

Hand it over with an implementation checklist and clear unresolved specialist referrals.

What is outside the scope?

This service is not legal advice, a statutory DPO service, DPIA sign-off, ISO 42001 implementation or certification, cyber penetration testing, employment-law drafting, technical system configuration or a guarantee of regulatory compliance.

If your organisation cannot yet tell us which AI tools or workflows staff actually use, a discovery-first service may be the better starting point.

Is this a good fit?

A good fit is an organisation already using AI where management can approve internal rules and needs a practical operating layer between high-level governance and everyday staff decisions.

It is not a good fit where the main need is disputed legal advice, formal certification, high-impact automated decisions about people, clinical or safeguarding use, technical security testing or a full enterprise AI-management transformation.
