AI Policy & SOP Implementation Service

Turn governance requirements into practical AI policy, acceptable-use rules, responsibilities and SOPs that can be implemented in day-to-day work.

What changes

The organisation moves from informal AI use to a practical operating framework: clear rules, named decision responsibilities and repeatable procedures for the agreed use cases.

The customer keeps its legal and accountability decisions; House of Carol turns approved requirements and actual working practices into usable governance rather than claiming certification or guaranteed compliance.

What you will receive

Tailored AI policy

A policy built around the organisation’s actual AI tools, practices and approved requirements, setting the operating rules and governance boundaries that staff need to follow.

Staff acceptable-use guide

A practical staff-facing guide that translates the policy into everyday decisions about permitted use, restricted use, information handling, checking and escalation.

Role-and-decision matrix

A clear map of who owns AI decisions, who may approve or operate agreed uses, and where questions or exceptions must be escalated.

Up to three operating SOPs

Up to three bounded procedures that turn the agreed policy into repeatable steps for the priority AI activities selected for the engagement.

Implementation checklist

An ordered list of the actions, owners and dependencies needed to put the agreed policy, guide, roles and SOPs into day-to-day use.

Manager action note

A concise note highlighting the decisions, communications and follow-up actions managers need to complete for the implementation to work in practice.

Handover

A structured handover explaining the agreed artefacts, ownership and escalation points so the organisation can maintain and apply them after the engagement.

What needs to be in place?

Customer decision owners.

Current authoritative requirements.

Accurate description of tools and practices.

Only the minimum information and access needed for the agreed work should be provided; passwords, MFA codes, private keys and unnecessary sensitive data are not requested through ordinary messages.

What is outside the scope?

No substitute for customer legal/accountability decisions.

No compliance guarantee or certification.

Anything materially outside the agreed boundary becomes a separate decision rather than hidden expansion.

Is this the right service?

This service is designed for SMEs, charities and associations using AI. It fits where the stated problem is real, the required inputs can be supplied and an accountable owner can make the necessary decisions.

If the main need requires a specialist judgement House of Carol is not competent or authorised to provide, a different route is required.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No revenue, saving, ranking, funding, procurement, productivity or other business outcome is guaranteed. Customer decisions and competent professional, legal, regulatory or ecclesiastical authority remain with the appropriate owner.
