The need

What problem does this address?

AI use exists without clear, usable organisational rules, responsibilities and operating procedures.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Turn governance requirements into practical AI policy, acceptable-use rules, responsibilities and SOPs that can be implemented in day-to-day work.

The work is delivered as a bounded professional service. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Tailored AI policy
  • staff acceptable-use guide
  • role-and-decision matrix
  • up to three operating SOPs
  • implementation checklist
  • manager action note
  • handover

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Confirm prerequisites, customer-owned access, data boundaries and acceptance criteria.
  2. Design and implement only the authorised bounded scope in the customer’s environment or agreed artefact set.
  3. Test the result, including fallback, recovery or exception handling where relevant.
  4. Complete quality, privacy and security checks that the exact facts trigger.
  5. Run customer acceptance and hand over the operating guidance, ownership and bounded remedy route.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One organisation; tailored policy/acceptable-use implementation with up to three operating SOPs.

What we need from you

  • Customer decision owners
  • current authoritative requirements
  • accurate description of tools and practices

Timing

Timing is confirmed after complete inputs and scope fit; no unsupported SLA is implied.

Boundaries

What is outside scope or not being promised?

Outside the standard scope

  • No substitute for customer legal/accountability decisions
  • no compliance guarantee or certification

No unsupported outcome claim

No guarantee of a particular business, funding, compliance, procurement, learning, revenue, productivity or other outcome is created beyond the exact bounded product record and evidence available.

Any wider or materially different work requires explicit re-scoping and may trigger additional specialist assurance.

Specialist boundary

AI, data and technology boundary

The service does not create legal, data-protection, cybersecurity or compliance certification. Exact tool, data, access and high-consequence facts can trigger Legal, Privacy or Security review and a STOP before work proceeds.
