Training Quality Review

Provide an evidence-based review of whether training is likely to achieve and measure the intended learning outcome.

Discuss the review

What changes

The customer receives a clear view of whether the training design, practice and assessment actually support the stated learning outcome — and which defects matter most.

The review distinguishes design evidence from claims about real-world learner performance; it does not treat a polished course or completion rate as proof of transfer.

What you will receive

Design review

Tests whether the objectives, content, sequence and learning activities line up with the capability the training is meant to build.

Learning-method critique

Examines whether learners get enough explanation, realistic practice, feedback and reinforcement rather than passive exposure alone.

Assessment review

Checks whether the assessment method actually measures the stated learning outcome and whether the evidence and criteria are clear.

Accessibility issues

Identifies practical barriers in materials, delivery or assessment and separates fixable design issues from specialist equality or legal questions.

Improvement actions

A prioritised set of specific corrections, with the most material design, assessment and accessibility issues first.
