Operations, process & governance

If work depends on chasing people, remembering what happens next or managers stepping in, the way the work is organised needs attention. These services make responsibilities, hand-offs and routines clearer.

Start with the problem you recognise. Each service page explains what we do, what you receive and what is not included.

SOP & Process Design Sprint

When: one recurring process varies between people, gets missed at hand-offs or keeps pulling a manager back in.

Sort out one recurring process: map how it works, make responsibilities clear, deal with exceptions and produce a practical SOP people can use.

Document Control & Knowledge-System Cleanup

Make it easier to find the right document and know it is current, with a simpler structure and less duplication. Nothing is deleted by default.

Turn the data you already collect into a small set of useful measures and a reporting view that supports actual management decisions.

Customer Journey & Service Operations Review

When: individual steps look reasonable but the overall customer journey feels fragmented.

Follow one customer journey from end to end, including the work and systems behind it, to show where it breaks down and what to fix first.

Customer Support Knowledge Base Build

When: customer support depends too much on whoever happens to know the answer.

Build a support knowledge base that people can find, update and trust, with clear ownership and escalation routes.

Business Continuity & Operational Readiness Pack

When: a critical service has dependencies or failure points but no proportionate fallback plan.

Identify the services, dependencies and failure points that matter most, then set out realistic fallback and recovery arrangements.

Decision Rights & Governance Review

When: decisions stall, duplicate or bounce upward because authority is unclear.

Make it clear who can decide what, when something needs to be escalated and which governance steps are actually useful.
