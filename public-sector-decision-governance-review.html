Public-Sector Decision Governance Review

Map and improve the operational governance of decisions while respecting the body's actual statutory and constitutional framework.

Decision routes, delegations and governance processes may be unclear or inefficient.

For: Public bodies and organisations supporting them.

Indicative £1,250–£2,500 per project. Final price depends on the evidenced public-sector context, authority/source boundary and agreed scope.

A defined advisory service centred on process map, authority clarity, evidence standards and the remaining listed deliverables. The exact boundary is confirmed before commitment.

Defined scope · evidence kept separate from assumptions · no unsupported outcome, certification or compliance claims

Discuss the review

What changes

The organisation gets a clearer operational picture of how the agreed decision route works, where authority is recorded and where process friction or ambiguity sits.

Improvement is constrained by the body’s actual framework; House of Carol does not invent statutory powers or legal interpretations.

What you will receive

Process map

A current-state map of the in-scope decision route, including the main stages, participants, papers, approvals and handoffs.

Authority clarity

A source-backed view of the relevant recorded delegations and decision roles, with legal interpretation referred rather than invented.

Evidence standards

A practical statement of the information, records and assurance the process needs at each material point.

Handoffs

Identification of delays, duplication and ambiguity where work moves between teams, officers, governance routes or decision-makers.

Improvement recommendations

Proportionate operational improvements that respect the body’s actual statutory and constitutional framework instead of designing around it.

What needs to be in place?

Current constitution/delegations/processes.

Competent public-body ownership.

Only the minimum information and access needed for the agreed work should be provided. Passwords, MFA codes, private keys and unnecessary confidential or personal data are not requested through ordinary messages.

What is outside the scope?

No statutory/public authority claimed.

No legal determination of powers beyond competence.

Anything materially outside the agreed boundary becomes a separate decision rather than hidden expansion of the engagement.

Is this the right service?

The organisation fits the intended customer: Public bodies and organisations supporting them.

There is an accountable customer owner able to provide the information and decisions the work depends on.

If the main need falls outside the stated deliverables or requires a specialist judgement House of Carol is not competent or authorised to provide, a different route is required.

What does House of Carol not promise?

The service does not promise a particular revenue, saving, ranking, award, funding decision, procurement outcome, productivity result or other business outcome unless that exact commitment is expressly part of the stated product — and none is implied here.

Customer decisions and competent professional, legal, regulatory or ecclesiastical authority remain with the appropriate owner.
