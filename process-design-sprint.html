SOP & Process Design Sprint

One important process, properly sorted out.

If work keeps getting missed between people, done differently depending on who handles it, or pulled back to the owner because nobody is quite sure what happens next, the problem is often the process itself.

House of Carol takes one recurring operational process, works out how it really runs, clears up weak handoffs and responsibilities, and turns it into a practical way of working your team can follow.

Best suited to: organisations where one recurring operational process crosses several people and has become difficult to run consistently, delegate or hand over.

Tell us about the process

Where a process starts to hurt

The work still gets done, but only because somebody keeps an eye on everything.

A new client says yes, but the handover into delivery varies each time.

Important information sits across emails, messages, spreadsheets and people’s memories.

Two colleagues describe the same process differently, and nobody is quite sure which version is right.

A new starter needs repeated explanation because the real method has never been made clear.

Something gets missed, a customer chases, or a manager has to step back in.

Growth or hiring makes an informal process that once felt harmless harder to sustain.

What changes

We start with what actually happens now, not what an old procedure says should happen.

The result is an agreed process boundary, clearer responsibilities and handoffs, defined decision points and a sensible route for important exceptions.

The aim is not to produce a handsome document describing the existing problem. It is to leave you with a clearer process that somebody else can understand and run.

What you will receive

A clear picture of the current process

We map how the work moves today and identify where ownership, information, decisions or handoffs become unclear.

An improved process

We design a clearer route, including responsibilities, important control points and what happens when the normal route does not apply.

A practical process map and SOP

You receive an improved process map and one usable SOP covering the agreed process, its normal steps, responsibilities and important exceptions.

A clear handover

We walk through the finished process with you and identify any implementation actions, dependencies or decisions that remain with your organisation.

How it works

The process is bounded before the work begins.

1. Define

We agree the process, its start and end points, the process owner and what a useful result needs to achieve.

2. Understand

We hold a remote discovery session of up to 90 minutes and review the minimum useful evidence needed to understand how the process really works.

3. Redesign

We identify avoidable friction, unclear ownership, weak handoffs, decision points and important exceptions, then design a clearer way of working.

4. Document

We turn the agreed improved process into a practical process map and SOP.

5. Hand over

We take you through the result in a remote handover of up to 60 minutes, identify your next actions and make any included in-scope correction.

What we need from you

You do not need to prepare a perfect procedure before we start. We need enough access and evidence to understand how the work really happens.

One process owner

A named person who understands the process, can explain it, coordinate factual corrections and confirm decisions.

Representative evidence

The minimum useful documents, screenshots or sample records needed to understand the current process, including important exceptions or failure points.

Your time at the key points

Up to 90 minutes for discovery, brief written clarification where reasonably needed, and up to 60 minutes for handover and review.

Fit and boundaries

A good fit

The process repeats regularly, crosses two or more people or roles, and causes recurring delay, rework, missed handoffs, difficult onboarding or regular owner or manager rescue.

Probably not worth £1,500

If one competent manager already understands a simple, stable, low-consequence procedure and can document it quickly, do it internally or use a cheaper template or diagnostic route.

Outside the standard sprint

Organisation-wide transformation, multiple unrelated processes, live system implementation, ongoing implementation management, and regulated, safety-critical or other high-consequence process redesign.

A defined engagement

The exact boundary, timetable and fee are confirmed before commitment.

What needs to be in place?

One named process owner, access to the minimum representative evidence needed to understand the process, and timely factual clarification where evidence conflicts.

You remain responsible for the accuracy of the information you supply and for deciding whether and how to implement the finished process.

What is outside the scope?

The sprint does not provide legal, regulatory, tax, safety, information-security or accreditation certification. It does not redesign regulated, safety-critical or other high-consequence processes.

It is also not the right service for organisation-wide transformation, multiple unrelated processes, live system implementation or ongoing implementation management.

Is this the right service?

It works best when the process happens repeatedly, involves more than one person or role, and has become dependent on memory, chasing or informal workarounds.

If the process is simple, stable and already understood well enough for one competent person to document it clearly, a £1,500 sprint is probably more than you need.

What does House of Carol not promise?

We do not promise a particular saving, ROI, productivity gain or commercial result. The sprint is process-design work, not legal, regulatory, tax, safety, information-security or accreditation certification.

Which process keeps pulling you back in?

Tell us what the process is, where it tends to go wrong and why it matters now. We will first check the fit and define the boundary. If it is simpler than this service requires, or falls outside the service boundary, we will say so before anything is agreed.
