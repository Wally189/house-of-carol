SOP & Process Design Sprint

Your business shouldn't depend on one person remembering how everything works.

When client work gets handed over differently every time, tasks are missed, people have to chase each other, or the owner keeps getting pulled back in, that is often a sign the process has never been made clear enough.

House of Carol takes one important workflow, works out how it really operates, fixes unclear handoffs and responsibilities, and turns it into a practical way of working your team can actually follow.

What happens during the sprint?

1. Define

Agree the process boundary, owner and useful result.

2. Understand

Review the minimum useful evidence and how work actually moves.

3. Redesign

Clarify handoffs, ownership, decisions, controls and important exceptions.

4. Document

Create the improved process map and one practical SOP.

5. Hand over

Walk through the result, identify customer-owned actions and correct one in-scope defect if needed.

What would you need from us?

One named process owner, a remote discovery session of up to 90 minutes, a remote handover/review of up to 60 minutes, and the minimum representative documents, screenshots or examples needed to understand the process.

Do not send passwords, credentials or unnecessary sensitive information. We work with the minimum useful evidence and will ask for redaction or anonymisation where appropriate.

What changes compared with the current process?

The aim is to move from memory-dependent handoffs, chasing, unclear ownership and improvised exceptions to one agreed boundary, clear owners and decision points, a defined route for normal work and awkward cases, and one practical process map and SOP.

The aim is not a polished document that sits in a folder. It is a process your team can understand, use and hand over without relying on one person's memory.

Is this the right service for our organisation?

The first-customer hypothesis is an owner-led England-and-Wales B2B agency, consultancy or similar non-regulated service business, normally 10–49 employees, with a recurring client-facing handoff that has become painful as the team grows.

A good fit is a recurring process involving two or more people, different versions of “how we do it”, missed or repeated tasks, difficult onboarding or delegation, or regular founder/manager chasing and rescue.

The broader UK market remains a future hypothesis; this wording does not claim proven demand.

This probably isn't worth £1,500 if…

The procedure is simple, stable, low-risk and already understood by one competent person who can document it easily.

In that case, a £1,500 sprint is probably more than you need. We would rather tell you that than sell work you do not need.

The standard sprint is also not designed for legal/compliance certification, regulated or high-consequence process redesign, organisation-wide transformation or live system implementation.
