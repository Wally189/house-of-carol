You may recognise this

The work gets done. It just takes too much chasing.

A new client says yes — and then everybody assumes somebody else has picked it up.

Useful information gets scattered across emails, messages, spreadsheets and somebody's memory.

Two people do the same task differently, and nobody is quite sure which version is the real process.

A new member of staff needs constant explanation because the actual way of working has never been made clear.

Something gets missed, the customer chases, and a manager or founder has to step in again.

Growth makes an informal process that once felt harmless start to become expensive in attention and time.

The difference

Before and after should be obvious.

Before

  • the handoff depends on memory;
  • people chase missing information;
  • ownership changes depending on who is around;
  • exceptions get improvised;
  • the owner or senior person becomes the safety net.

After

  • one agreed process boundary;
  • clear owners, handoffs and decision points;
  • a defined route for the normal job and the awkward cases;
  • one practical process map and SOP;
  • a clearer basis for delegation and onboarding.

What you get

We fix the process before we write the procedure.

If you already have a simple process that works well and only needs typing up, you probably do not need us.

The value here is in finding out what actually happens — not what everybody thinks happens — and making the process better before it is documented.

  • an evidence-based view of how the process works today;
  • the main friction, duplication, unclear ownership and weak handoffs identified;
  • a clearer future-state process;
  • explicit responsibilities and decision points;
  • important exceptions and failure routes, not just the happy path;
  • one practical step-by-step SOP aligned to the improved process;
  • a handover showing what changes next;
  • one correction cycle for anything agreed but missing or wrong.

The aim is not a polished document that sits in a folder. The aim is a process your team can understand, use and hand over without relying on one person's memory.

How it works

One bounded job from messy to usable.

01

Define

We agree exactly which process is in scope, who owns it and what a useful result needs to achieve.

02

Understand

We speak with the process owner and review the minimum useful evidence to see how the work really moves today.

03

Redesign

We clarify handoffs, ownership, decisions, controls and the important exceptions.

04

Document

We turn the improved route into a practical process map and one step-by-step SOP your team can use.

05

Hand over

We walk through the result, identify the actions that remain with you and correct one in-scope defect if needed.

What we need from you

A small amount of the right input.

You need one named process owner who can explain what happens and answer reasonable follow-up questions.

The standard sprint includes a remote discovery session of up to 90 minutes and a remote handover/review of up to 60 minutes, plus the minimum representative documents, screenshots or examples needed to understand the process.

Please do not send passwords, credentials or unnecessary sensitive information. We work with the minimum useful evidence and will ask for redaction or anonymisation where appropriate.

Delivery is normally within 10 working days once the agreed inputs are available. If your input is delayed, the delivery clock pauses rather than creating a false deadline.

Fit

Worth buying only when the problem is real.

A good fit

  • the same process happens regularly;
  • two or more people are involved;
  • people describe the process differently;
  • tasks get missed, delayed or repeated;
  • a founder or manager regularly has to chase or intervene;
  • onboarding or delegation is harder than it should be;
  • growth or hiring means the current way of working no longer scales.

Probably not a good fit

If the procedure is simple, stable, low-risk and already understood by one competent person who can document it easily, a £1,500 sprint is probably more than you need.

We would rather tell you that than sell you work you do not need.

The standard sprint is also not designed for legal or compliance certification, regulated or high-consequence process redesign, organisation-wide transformation or live system implementation.
