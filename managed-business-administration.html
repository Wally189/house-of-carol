Managed Business Administration

Keep recurring administration moving without turning it into unlimited assistant access.

Use this when routine administration is not a one-off project, but it is important enough that missed updates, stale records, calendar preparation or follow-up keep pulling the owner back in.

We agree a small set of recurring workflows, one intake route and clear limits so the work can keep moving without quietly becoming an open-ended virtual-assistant service.

For: owner-led B2B service businesses in England and Wales, typically with 1–10 people, that need reliable recurring administration around work they already understand.

£950per month

One controlled intake. Up to three agreed recurring workflows, with a maximum eight hours of House of Carol capacity each month and no carry-over.

Remote recurring service. The exact workflows, access, timing and fit are agreed before anything starts.

Routine administration only. No unlimited assistant access, unattended commitments, payments or specialist professional work.

What changes

Routine work has a defined home, rhythm and stopping point.

One intake replaces scattered chasing. Agreed recurring requests come through one controlled route rather than several informal channels.

Routine records stay attended to. The agreed trackers, registers, actions or document routines are maintained within the monthly scope.

Exceptions come back to the right person. Decisions, unclear instructions and out-of-scope requests are returned rather than guessed at.

The service remains finite. The monthly boundary makes it clear what is included, what waits and what needs a different service.

What you will receive

Choose up to three recurring workflow options for the monthly service.

Meeting and action administration

Prepare agreed meeting materials, maintain action lists, record routine updates and queue follow-up for the named customer owner.

Document and records administration

Maintain agreed records, indexes, filing routines or document-status updates using the structure and authority the customer has already set.

Tracker, CRM or register maintenance

Keep agreed fields, statuses and routine entries current from authorised information supplied through the controlled intake.

Scheduling and calendar preparation

Prepare agreed scheduling options, calendars, reminders or meeting arrangements for customer approval where approval is needed.

Routine correspondence preparation

Prepare or queue routine correspondence from approved facts and instructions. External sending or substantive commitments remain with the authorised customer unless separately agreed.

Monthly status review

A concise monthly view of completed recurring work, open items, exceptions, decisions needed and any pressure on the agreed capacity.

Controlled handover

A clear record of the agreed routines and current state so the service can be changed or stopped without trapping knowledge inside House of Carol.

A defined engagement

The monthly boundary is agreed before work starts.

This is not an unlimited-hours retainer. If the agreed work no longer fits the monthly boundary, we stop, make the pressure visible and agree the appropriate next step before widening anything.

What needs to be in place?

One named accountable customer owner, clearly defined workflows and sources, accurate authorised inputs, timely approvals or exception decisions, and only the access genuinely needed for the agreed work.

If a workflow later needs automation or system-owner permissions, that is assessed separately. House of Carol does not assume administrator access by default.

What is outside the scope?

Unlimited VA or EA support, 24/7 or urgent live-phone cover, travel, purchasing, spending or payment execution, bookkeeping, payroll, tax or statutory accounts, debt enforcement, HR, legal, clinical, safeguarding or other regulated judgement are outside this service.

Destructive file deletion or migration, KPI design, process redesign, customer-system administration, unattended external sending, permission changes and substantive commitments are also outside the standard monthly service.

Is this the right service?

It fits when the recurring work is already understood and the problem is keeping a small set of routine administrative workflows moving reliably.

If the underlying process itself is unclear, use the SOP & Process Design Sprint. If the document estate needs structural cleanup, use Document Control & Knowledge-System Cleanup. If management measures need designing, use Management Information & KPI Setup.

What does House of Carol not promise?

The service does not promise instant responses, unlimited availability, a particular saving, productivity gain or business result. It provides defined recurring administration within the agreed monthly capacity and relies on timely, accurate customer inputs and decisions.
