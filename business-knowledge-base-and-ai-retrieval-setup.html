Business Knowledge Base & AI Retrieval Setup

Create a governed source structure and retrieval layer so staff can find and use current organisational knowledge more reliably.

Organisational knowledge is scattered, duplicated or hard to retrieve reliably.

For: SMEs and charities.

£1,500 standard — indicative scope band £1,000–£2,250. Final price depends on the agreed repository/retrieval pattern and bounded scope.

One bounded knowledge domain/repository covering source/current-state assessment, source-of-truth and ownership decisions, knowledge architecture/taxonomy, repository structure, permissions/access model, retrieval configuration where supported, operating rules, migration plan, handover and acceptance tests.

Source truth first · permissions visible · retrieval tested · no claim that AI retrieval is complete or error-free

What changes

The organisation moves from scattered knowledge towards a governed source model and retrieval route with ownership, permissions, stale/conflict handling and update responsibility visible.

The supported repository and retrieval pattern must fit the customer before implementation is agreed.

What you will receive

Knowledge architecture

Source-of-truth, ownership, taxonomy and lifecycle decisions for the agreed domain.

Source cleanup and repository structure

A controlled structure and migration plan rather than destructive movement by assumption.

Access model

Permissions and access responsibilities made explicit.

Retrieval configuration

Retrieval configuration where the agreed platform/pattern is supported.

Operating rules and acceptance tests

Rules for maintenance plus tests for traceability, access, relevance, stale/conflict handling and fallback.

What needs to be in place?

Customer content and clear source ownership.

Permissions and a secure platform/access route.

An accountable owner for update and acceptance decisions.

What is outside the scope?

No promise that AI retrieval is complete or error-free.

Sensitive data requires appropriate controls.

Unsupported platforms or destructive migration are not silently included.

Is this the right service?

It fits an SME or charity with one knowledge domain where current information is scattered or hard to retrieve and where ownership and permissions can be made explicit.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No completeness, perfect retrieval, zero-error or permanent accuracy claim is made. The knowledge owner remains responsible for current authoritative content.
