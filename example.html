Fictional worked example

The job is finished. The invoice is still waiting.

This example shows the sort of operational defect House of Carol looks for. It is not a real customer case, testimonial, measured saving or claim that payment would arrive sooner.

The software is not broken.

The accounting package can raise an invoice perfectly well. The missing part is the hand-off that tells the business the work is billable and ready for the next action.

As found.

A small service business completes a job on Tuesday. The invoice is not raised until Monday because the process between those two events relies on memory and email.

01 · ACCEPTED

The quote becomes a job.

Customer and price information already exist, but the working job record lives elsewhere.

02 · DELIVERED

The work finishes.

The person doing the job knows it is complete, but “finished and billable” is not a shared state.

03 · HAND-OFF

An email carries the baton.

The owner hears about completion when somebody remembers to tell them.

04 · INVOICE

The job is reconstructed.

The owner checks the quote, retypes information and raises the invoice days later.

The breakpoint

The business has no reliable ready-to-invoice state.

The defect sits upstream of payment chasing. The owner has become the integration between delivery and billing.

What that causes

Routine work needs human memory.

  • “finished” and “ready to invoice” mean different things but are not distinguished;
  • an email substitutes for a controlled transition;
  • customer and job information is reconstructed;
  • ordinary and exceptional jobs are mixed together;
  • status has to be asked for rather than seen.

The repair.

The smallest useful Better State is not “buy another system”. It is to define the state change, carry the minimum information once and surface the exceptions that genuinely need attention.

01Define.

Agree exactly what makes a job ready to invoice and who owns that transition.

02Carry.

Move the minimum customer, job and price reference from the authoritative source rather than rebuilding it later.

03Surface.

Make ready, invoiced, paid and exception states visible enough that ordinary work does not require another chase.

04Automate only if earned.

Use a form, configuration change or lightweight automation only where it beats the simpler operating solution.
