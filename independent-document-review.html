What do you check?

We check whether important factual claims are supported; whether statistics, quotations, dates and attributions stand up; whether the source actually supports the wording used; whether conclusions follow from the evidence; and whether the document contains hidden assumptions, contradictions, reasoning gaps, overclaiming or important contrary evidence.

This is not proofreading. We are not mainly looking for commas or prettier sentences.

What happens during the review?

1. Define

Agree the exact version, audience, intended use and material sources.

2. Check

Test the important facts, sources and evidence rather than assuming the draft is right.

3. Challenge

Look for weak reasoning, hidden assumptions, contradictions and overclaiming.

4. Prioritise

Provide a clear findings list showing what matters, why it matters and what to do next.

5. Re-check

Re-check the revised version once while the agreed scope stays the same.

What would you need from us?

The final or near-final document, who will see it, what it is meant to achieve, the main source material, and confirmation that you are authorised to share it.

Do not send passwords, credentials or unnecessary personal or sensitive information. Confidential or personal material is handled only where the appropriate data, security and contractual route is in place.

Is this the right service for our organisation?

It is designed for accountable people in small and medium UK organisations who have to stand behind an important document after it leaves their desk — including owners and managing directors, bid and proposal leads, operations, strategy or governance leads, and research, communications or editorial leads.

AI-assisted drafting is one relevant trigger, not a qualification requirement. The service is the same whether AI wrote part of the first draft or none of it.

This is not legal sign-off, clinical or safety-critical review, regulated financial advice, statutory audit or certification, penetration testing or full AI-system assurance.

This probably isn't worth £750 if…
