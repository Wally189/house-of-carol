AI Output Assurance & Red-Team Review

Challenge important AI-assisted output before somebody else finds the weakness first.

AI-assisted work may contain factual, evidential, reasoning, safety or boundary failures even when the finished output looks convincing.

For: Organisations producing material AI-assisted outputs.

£750 fixed fee

One defined document up to 5,000 words or one presentation up to 20 slides, for one stated audience and intended use, with up to 10 material source items and one bounded re-check.

Planned delivery: normally within three working days once the complete agreed pack is received.

Evidence challenged, not rubber-stamped · uncertainty stays visible · no certification claim or promise that every possible error will be found

Discuss the review

What changes

You get a structured challenge of the material facts, sources, reasoning and boundaries in the output before it is relied on or released.

Weak evidence, contradictions and uncertainty remain visible rather than being polished away.

What you will receive

Source and evidence check

Check whether material claims are actually supported by the supplied sources.

Adversarial review

Challenge reasoning, hidden assumptions, contradictions and overclaiming.

Defect register

A prioritised record of material defects and why they matter.

Corrections

Bounded correction recommendations where the evidence supports them.

Residual-limit statement

A clear statement of what remains uncertain or outside the review boundary.

What needs to be in place?

The final or near-final output, intended audience and intended use.

The material source set needed to test the important claims.

Confirmation that the customer is authorised to share the material.

Passwords, credentials and unnecessary personal or sensitive information are not required.

What is outside the scope?

No regulated certification.

No guarantee that the review eliminates all error.

No legal, clinical, safety-critical, regulated financial or other specialist sign-off outside competent scope.

Is this the right service?

It fits material AI-assisted work where an independent challenge of facts, sources and reasoning is proportionate to the consequence of an escaped defect.

If a competent colleague can check the output adequately and the consequences of error are small, the review may not be worth buying.

What does House of Carol not promise?

The review does not promise that every possible error will be found or that a regulator, court, client, board, funder or other third party will accept the output.
