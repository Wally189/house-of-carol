About House of Carol

A venture business, not a catalogue of promises.

House of Carol is an owner-operated British business based in Bristol. Its purpose is to develop lawful commercial ventures that solve real problems and can earn their place through evidence.

What that means

The parent business stays stable while individual ideas are tested.

A promising idea can become a service, product, research asset, publication, partnership or another kind of commercial venture. It does not become part of the public offer simply because it sounds clever.

House of Carol investigates the problem, defines what would count as useful, develops the smallest credible proposition and tests it. Evidence determines whether the idea is retained, changed, expanded or stopped.

What exists today

The two propositions currently developed far enough to explain on this website are Workflow Implementation and Website Release Assurance. Their pages state their present scope and limitations. They are current ventures, not a claim that the House will permanently be a consultancy.

Why the language is cautious

The business is still in commissioning. We therefore distinguish demonstrated capability and internal proof from external customer evidence. We do not invent customer numbers, testimonials, quantified savings, premises, staff or maturity that do not exist.

Technology is subordinate to the result

House of Carol uses modern tools, including AI where appropriate, but the customer should not have to buy a technology ideology. A tool earns its place by making the result better, safer, faster or more economical.
