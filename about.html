About House of Carol

Good work often starts with sorting out the mess.

Small organisations face plenty of important jobs that do not fit neatly into somebody's job title: research that needs doing properly, information that needs untangling, digital work that needs checking, opportunities that need assessing and processes that have become harder than they ought to be.

House of Carol exists for that kind of work.

The idea

Useful help, without unnecessary theatre.

A small organisation does not always need a large consultancy, another software subscription or a permanent specialist. Sometimes it needs somebody to understand a defined problem, do the work carefully and return something genuinely useful.

House of Carol combines research, practical technology and human judgement to do exactly that. Individual products and services are deliberately bounded; the wider House gives us room to investigate different kinds of business problem without pretending every problem has the same solution.

Founder

Alan W Gallagher

Alan is a Bristol-based business and technical operations professional with more than twenty years' experience across IT services, information security administration, healthcare, customer operations, legal administration and regulated service environments.

His career has repeatedly put him between technical teams, managers, vendors, operational users and customers: clarifying requirements, coordinating delivery, resolving service problems, improving documentation and keeping complicated work moving.

His experience includes enterprise access and information-security administration, hardware procurement and lifecycle work, business and technical liaison, customer operations, sales administration, healthcare operations, process improvement and governance support. He has worked through major operational change including Brexit transition activity and pandemic remote-working deployment.

The common thread is less glamorous and more useful: understanding how work actually moves, noticing where it gets stuck, translating technical detail into practical action and leaving behind a clearer record than the one he inherited.

Working approach

A bit of mess to sort out?

Alan's own description is straightforward: he is useful where there are unclear processes, competing priorities, technical detail that needs translating, or work that needs a proper trail of actions, owners and decisions.

The House applies the same instinct commercially: clarify the problem, understand the workflow, separate facts from assumptions, create the structure the job needs and keep moving towards a finished result.

How we work

Professional where it matters. Human everywhere else.

Understand first. We start with what you are actually trying to achieve.

Agree the job. Scope, inputs, outputs and boundaries should be understandable before work begins.

Do the work carefully. Technology can make work faster; it does not remove the need for judgement.

Show what we found. Important conclusions should have a trail back to the information behind them.

Leave things better organised. A useful engagement should reduce confusion, not manufacture dependency.
