Tender Decision & Submission Review

A fresh pair of eyes before an important submission goes in.

House of Carol can review a substantially complete tender response to identify unanswered questions, weak evidence, avoidable ambiguity and points that deserve another look before submission.

The review challenges the response; it does not replace the bidder’s own judgement, write unsupported claims or pretend to know what an evaluator will decide.

This page describes a service candidate being prepared for the 2 October 2026 launch. External commercial proof is absent. Scope, timing, price and handling of customer material must be confirmed before any engagement.

01

Read what was asked

Check the submission against the requirement rather than against what the response hoped the requirement meant.

02

Test what is claimed

Look for evidence gaps, unclear wording and statements that may need strengthening or qualification.

03

Find what was missed

Surface omissions, unanswered points and internal contradictions that are easy to stop seeing after repeated drafting.

04

Prioritise what matters

Separate material issues from minor polish so effort goes to the changes most worth making.
