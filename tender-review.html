Tender Decision & Submission Review

Give a substantially complete tender an evidence-honest final challenge against the supplied requirements and evaluation criteria before submission.

Bidders need an independent structured challenge of a substantially completed tender before submission.

For: SMEs and VCSEs bidding for contracts.

£850 standard — indicative scope band £750–£950. Final price is confirmed after scope fit.

One substantially complete tender for one opportunity or lot, using the authoritative requirement/evaluation source set, with one structured review, prioritised correction register, submission-readiness note and bounded factual/misreading correction cycle.

Requirements and evidence challenged · no bid writing from scratch · no predicted score or award guarantee

Discuss the tender

What changes

The bid owner gets a structured view of requirement gaps, weak evidence, unsupported claims and material submission risks before the bidder decides what to correct.

House of Carol does not take over authorship, portal submission or the customer's evidence obligations.

What you will receive

Review baseline and source register

Freeze the draft and authoritative tender/evaluation source set being reviewed.

Requirement/evaluation map

Map the supplied requirements and evaluation criteria against the submission.

Submission review register

A structured record of findings and evidence issues.

Unsupported-claim list

Claims that need evidence, qualification or removal.

Prioritised corrections

Critical, material and minor corrections separated so the bid owner can act proportionately.

Submission-readiness note

A concise readiness assessment without predicting score or award.

Readout and bounded correction cycle

Up to 45-minute readout plus one bounded factual/misreading correction cycle.

A defined engagement

Scope
One opportunity/lot and one substantially complete frozen draft, normally received early enough to preserve customer correction time before deadline.

The bidder remains responsible for truthful evidence, final drafting, portal submission, signatures and the procurement decision.

What needs to be in place?

One accountable bid owner.

A substantially complete frozen draft for one opportunity or lot.

The exact authoritative tender/evaluation source set.

Truthful evidence, customer authority for third-party review and appropriate confidentiality/data controls.

What is outside the scope?

No bid writing from scratch or unlimited rewriting.

No fabricated evidence.

No procurement-law opinion, portal credentials/submission/signatures or multiple lots/opportunities in the standard unit.

No predicted score, award or revenue guarantee.

Is this the right service?

It fits a bidder with one substantially complete tender and enough time to act on a structured independent review.

If the bid still needs to be written from scratch, this is not the standard service.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No score, award, procurement-law compliance or revenue outcome is guaranteed. The bidder owns the final submission and evidence.
