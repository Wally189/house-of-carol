The need

What problem does this address?

Bidders need an independent structured challenge of a substantially completed tender before submission.

The service

What will House of Carol actually do?

Give a substantially complete tender an evidence-honest final challenge against the supplied requirements and evaluation criteria so the bidder can decide what to correct before submission without outsourcing the bid.

The work is delivered as a bounded professional service. The exact scope is agreed before commitment and does not transfer the customer’s own decision-making authority to House of Carol.

Deliverables

What will you receive?

  • Review baseline/source register
  • requirement/evaluation map
  • submission review register
  • evidence and unsupported-claim list
  • prioritised critical/material/minor corrections
  • submission-readiness note
  • up to 45-minute readout
  • one bounded factual/misreading correction cycle

Method

How does the work happen?

  1. Define the question, opportunity or funding/tender requirement and verify scope or eligibility.
  2. Gather current authoritative evidence and record sources, assumptions and limitations.
  3. Analyse the evidence against the declared criteria without fabricating missing facts.
  4. Quality-check citations, claims and material qualifications.
  5. Complete factual review and hand over the decision-ready output; submission or external contact remains separately authorised.

Scope, inputs and timing

What is within scope, what do we need from you, and how long should it take?

Standard boundary

One substantially complete tender for one opportunity/lot, using the frozen authoritative tender and evaluation source set.

What we need from you

  • One accountable bid owner
  • substantially complete frozen draft
  • one opportunity/lot
  • exact authoritative tender/evaluation source set
  • accepted complete pack normally at least three working days before deadline so two-working-day review plus one full customer-correction day remain
  • truthful evidence
  • customer authority for third-party review
  • conflict check
  • approved confidentiality/data/security route
  • HOC-024 PI/residual-risk proprietor decision
  • separate explicit external buyer-test authority

Timing

The standard review requires the complete accepted pack normally at least three working days before the tender deadline.

Boundaries

What is outside scope or not being promised?

Outside the standard scope

  • No bid writing from scratch or unlimited rewriting
  • no fabricated evidence
  • no procurement-law/legal opinion
  • no portal credentials/submission/signatures
  • no multiple lots/opportunities in standard unit
  • no predicted score/award/revenue guarantee
  • no specialist technical/clinical/safety/regulatory judgement beyond competence
  • no same-procurement conflicted role
  • no unnecessary personal/special-category/security-sensitive data
  • no customer indemnity/unlimited liability in standard candidate

No unsupported outcome claim

No guarantee of a particular business, funding, compliance, procurement, learning, revenue, productivity or other outcome is created beyond the exact bounded product record and evidence available.

Any wider or materially different work requires explicit re-scoping and may trigger additional specialist assurance.

Specialist boundary

Commercial and regulated-activity boundary

Customer authority remains with the customer. Accounting, tax, regulated credit, legal enforcement, public-procurement or other regulated matters are outside the ordinary service unless separately competent and authorised.
