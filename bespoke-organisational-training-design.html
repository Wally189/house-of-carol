What changes

The learning design is anchored to the actual performance requirement rather than to a generic course format, with objectives, practice and assessment designed as one coherent system.

The service stays inside the defined learner group, source basis and agreed capability boundary; final timing and effort are confirmed before commitment.

What you will receive

Learning objectives

Clear objectives derived from the defined performance requirement so the intended learning can be designed and checked against a real capability need.

Programme architecture

A coherent structure showing how the agreed learning content, practice and assessment fit together for the defined learner group.

Materials

Learning materials built from the agreed source basis and organised to support the objectives and programme structure.

Practice

Applied practice activities aligned to the capability being developed rather than passive content exposure alone.

Assessment plan

A proportionate plan for checking whether learners can demonstrate the intended learning outcome, without claiming a regulated credential.

Facilitator guidance

Practical guidance for delivering the agreed programme consistently, including how the learning activities, practice and assessment connect.

Scope

What needs to be in place?

Defined learner group.

Defined performance requirement.

Appropriate source basis for the content.

What is outside the scope?

No accredited qualification or regulated credential claim.

Anything materially outside the agreed learning and capability boundary becomes a separate decision.

Is this the right service?

This service is designed for employers and charities that know what capability people need but require a coherent way to teach and assess it.

If the requested training requires regulated accreditation or specialist authority outside House of Carol’s competence, a different route is required.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No learner-performance, productivity or business outcome is guaranteed. Internal design evidence is not represented as external customer or market proof.
