Committee / Board Paper Quality Review

Apply a structured review to improve decision framing, evidence, risk visibility and actionability.

Papers do not consistently support clear, evidenced and actionable decisions.

For: Public, charity and corporate boards.

Defined scope · evidence kept separate from assumptions · no unsupported outcome, certification or compliance claims

Discuss the review

What changes

The paper becomes more decision-useful: the ask is clearer, material evidence and uncertainty are visible, and the actions or consequences are easier to follow.

The review challenges the paper; it does not replace the organisation’s legal, governance-officer or formal decision sign-off.

What you will receive

Paper review

A whole-paper review against the stated purpose, intended decision, audience and relevant governance context.

Decision framing

A check that the ask, options, recommendation and decision authority are visible rather than buried in background material.

Evidence critique

A challenge of material claims, sources, assumptions and contrary evidence so weak support is not hidden by polished prose.

Risk/actionability comments

Focused comments on material risks, dependencies, mitigations, ownership and what happens after the decision.

Prioritised revisions

A short revision register separating critical decision-quality defects from material improvements and minor presentational points.

Is this the right service?
