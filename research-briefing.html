Research & Evidence Briefing Service

One important question. A clear, sourced answer that keeps uncertainty visible.

Decision-makers need trustworthy, concise synthesis rather than unsourced or overwhelming information.

For: Businesses, charities and decision-makers.

£900 fixed fee

One defined decision question, normally a 1,500–3,000-word briefing with up to 20 material sources where proportionate, one agreed geography/timeframe and one decision context.

Planned delivery: Normally within five working days once the question and inputs are agreed.

Defined scope · evidence kept separate from assumptions · no unsupported outcome, certification or compliance claims

What changes

The customer gets one decision-ready answer rather than a pile of links: key evidence, competing evidence, uncertainty and implications are brought together in one bounded briefing.

If the sources do not support a confident conclusion, the briefing says so rather than inventing certainty.

What you will receive

Research question

A precise statement of the question, audience, geography, timeframe and decision context so the research has a real boundary.

Source review

A review of the strongest relevant current authoritative and high-quality sources found within the agreed scope.

Synthesis

A concise answer that brings the material evidence together for the decision rather than reproducing a search archive.

Competing evidence

Important evidence pointing in a different direction, including credible disagreement between sources.

Uncertainty

Visible limitations, evidence gaps and questions the research cannot resolve confidently.

Implications

Practical implications and decision questions that follow from the evidence without pretending to provide professional advice outside competence.

Source list

A traceable list of the material sources so the customer can inspect where important claims came from.

What needs to be in place?

Defined question.

Competent specialist domain.

Current authoritative sources.

Only the minimum information and access needed for the agreed work should be provided. Passwords, MFA codes, private keys and unnecessary confidential or personal data are not requested through ordinary messages.

What is outside the scope?

No professional opinion outside competence.

No fabricated primary evidence.

Anything materially outside the agreed boundary becomes a separate decision rather than hidden expansion of the engagement.

Is this the right service?

There is one defined decision question, audience and decision context.

The subject can be researched within a competent source boundary.

If the main need falls outside the stated deliverables or requires a specialist judgement House of Carol is not competent or authorised to provide, a different route is required.

What does House of Carol not promise?

The service does not promise a particular revenue, saving, ranking, award, funding decision, procurement outcome, productivity result or other business outcome unless that exact commitment is expressly part of the stated product — and none is implied here.

Customer decisions and competent professional, legal, regulatory or ecclesiastical authority remain with the appropriate owner.
