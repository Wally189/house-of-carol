Privacy

Privacy information.

This controlled-preview website does not currently contain a public email form, analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies.

If you contact House of Carol

If you choose to contact House of Carol by an available route, information you provide may be used to deal with your enquiry, manage any resulting business relationship and keep proportionate records.

What we ask for

Please provide only what is reasonably needed for the matter. Sensitive or confidential information should not be supplied unless it is genuinely necessary and an appropriate route has been agreed.

Sharing and retention

House of Carol does not sell enquiry information. Information may be shared with a service provider where that is necessary to operate an agreed service, or disclosed where required by law or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal rights. Records are kept only for as long as there is a proper business, legal or record-keeping reason.

Your rights

UK data-protection law can give individuals rights over their personal information depending on the circumstances. A privacy request can be raised using the current House contact route and should make clear that it concerns personal information.
