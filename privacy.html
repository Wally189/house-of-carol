Privacy.

Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol is responsible for the personal information described in this notice.

Website enquiries

If you use the House of Carol contact form, we collect your name, email address and the information you put in your message. We use that information to understand and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to take steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract.

We do not use contact-form information for unrelated marketing without an appropriate legal basis.

Form provider

The contact form is processed using Formspree. Formspree stores form submissions in the Formspree account and can deliver notifications to the configured recipient. Formspree states that its services are hosted using Amazon Web Services in the United States and that it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses as a data processor for GDPR transfers.

How long we keep enquiries

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is needed for the enquiry, any resulting business relationship, required records, or the handling of a dispute or legal obligation. Enquiries that do not proceed are reviewed periodically and deleted when there is no continuing reason to keep them.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently run advertising trackers or non-essential analytics. If that changes, this notice and any consent mechanism will be updated before those technologies are used where required.

Your information

You can ask about the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and exercise other applicable data-protection rights. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
