Privacy notice

How we use personal information.

Last updated: 12 September 2026.

Who is responsible

Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol is the data controller. Postal contact: 116 Knole Lane, Bristol, BS10 6RY, United Kingdom. The customer-facing email identity is alan@houseofcarol.co.uk.

The website enquiry form and branded email receiving route are currently being commissioned and must not be treated as active website submission routes until the site states otherwise.

Current website enquiry state

The website enquiry form is currently disabled while its end-to-end receiving route is verified. It does not currently submit enquiry details to Formspree or another website-form processor.

What we collect and why

When an authorised business enquiry route is active, House of Carol may collect the name, email address, message and other information that you choose to provide so that we can read, respond to and administer the enquiry. Please do not provide unnecessary sensitive information, passwords, credentials or confidential files at the initial-contact stage.

For ordinary business enquiries, our expected lawful basis is legitimate interests in receiving and responding to relevant enquiries. The legitimate interest is operating the business and answering people who choose to contact it. Where you ask us to take steps before entering into a contract, processing may also be necessary for that purpose. We do not add enquiry details to a marketing list without a separate lawful basis.

Who receives the information

No website form submission is currently transmitted to a form processor. Before an online enquiry route is activated, this notice will be checked against the actual receiving route and will identify any processor and any relevant international-transfer information required for that live route. Email or related records may be processed by the provider used for an authorised email route.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage any resulting business relationship, meet legal or record-keeping duties, or resolve a dispute. Enquiries that lead nowhere are reviewed and deleted when they are no longer useful or required.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your information and, where the legal conditions apply, other rights under UK data-protection law. Contact us to exercise a right. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Right to object

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you have the right to object to that processing. We must stop unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is needed for legal claims. The right to object to direct marketing is absolute.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. If that changes, this notice and any necessary consent mechanism will be updated before use.
