Privacy notice

How we use personal information.

Last updated: 19 September 2026.

Who is responsible

Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol is the data controller. Postal contact: 116 Knole Lane, Bristol, BS10 6RY, United Kingdom. The customer-facing email identity is alan@houseofcarol.co.uk.

The website enquiry and introductory-call request forms are processed through Formspree, which provides the form-processing and delivery route used by House of Carol.

Current website enquiry state

The website enquiry and introductory-call request forms submit the information you enter to Formspree for processing and delivery to House of Carol. A call request is not a confirmed appointment; House of Carol will confirm the date and time separately.

What we collect and why

When you use the website enquiry form, House of Carol may collect your name, email address, message and other information that you choose to provide so that we can read, respond to and administer the enquiry.

When you request an introductory call, House of Carol may collect your name, email address, telephone number and preferred call time so that we can respond to the request and arrange the call.

Please do not provide unnecessary sensitive information, passwords, credentials or confidential files at the initial-contact stage.

For ordinary business enquiries and call requests, our expected lawful basis is legitimate interests in receiving and responding to relevant enquiries and arranging conversations requested by the person contacting us. The legitimate interest is operating the business and answering people who choose to contact it. Where you ask us to take steps before entering into a contract, processing may also be necessary for that purpose. We do not add enquiry or call-request details to a marketing list without a separate lawful basis.

Who receives the information

Formspree, Inc. acts as the website-form processor for enquiries and introductory-call requests submitted through this site. Formspree's published security information states that its service is hosted using Amazon Web Services in the United States and that it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses as a data processor. This means form submissions may be processed outside the United Kingdom.

Email or related records may also be processed by the provider used for an authorised email route. We do not sell enquiry or call-request information or use it for unrelated marketing without a separate lawful basis.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and call requests only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, arrange the requested conversation, manage any resulting business relationship, meet legal or record-keeping duties, or resolve a dispute. Enquiries and requests that lead nowhere are reviewed and deleted when they are no longer useful or required.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your information and, where the legal conditions apply, other rights under UK data-protection law. Contact us to exercise a right. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Right to object

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you have the right to object to that processing. We must stop unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is needed for legal claims. The right to object to direct marketing is absolute.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. If that changes, this notice and any necessary consent mechanism will be updated before use.
