Privacy

House of Carol collects only the personal information needed for the activity you choose to use.

Website enquiries

If you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address and the information you put in your message. We use this information to understand and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to take steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract. For general business enquiries and website security we may also rely on our legitimate interests, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

We do not use contact-form information for unrelated marketing without an appropriate legal basis.

Form provider

Our website contact form is processed using Formspree. Formspree processes submissions on our behalf. Formspree states that its services are hosted using Amazon Web Services in the United States and that it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses for GDPR transfers as a data processor.

How long we keep enquiries

Enquiries that do not become an active customer or business relationship are normally deleted when they are no longer needed and reviewed no later than 12 months after the last substantive contact. If an enquiry becomes a customer relationship, relevant records may be retained for longer where needed for the contract, accounting, tax, dispute or other legal purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently run advertising trackers or non-essential analytics. If that changes, this notice and any consent mechanism will be updated before those technologies are used where required.
