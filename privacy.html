Privacy

Privacy information.

This page explains how Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol handles personal information provided through business correspondence and use of this website.

Website tracking

This website does not currently use advertising trackers, analytics, fingerprinting or non-essential cookies. The site does not contain a contact form. External services reached through links have their own practices.

Email enquiries

If you email House of Carol you will normally provide an email address, your message and any information you choose to include. We use that information to read and respond to the enquiry, assess fit, manage any resulting business relationship and keep proportionate business records.

Keep information proportionate

Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information by ordinary email. If work genuinely requires access credentials, confidential material or personal data, the minimum necessary handling arrangement is agreed separately before that element begins.

Sharing and processors

House of Carol does not sell enquiry information. Business email currently operates through Gmail/Google services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights. Any additional processor needed for a customer engagement is considered against the actual work rather than assumed in advance.

Retention

Enquiry and customer information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, any resulting relationship, support, accounting/tax or other legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Your rights and privacy contact

UK data-protection law can give you rights over personal information depending on the circumstances. For a privacy question or relevant rights request, email the House of Carol Privacy Office at alanwgallagher1@gmail.com.
