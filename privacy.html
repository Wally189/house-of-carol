Privacy

Privacy information.

This page explains how Alan W Gallagher trading as House of Carol handles information submitted through this website.

What this website collects

If you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address and message. The form also contains a hidden anti-spam field.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to read and respond to your message, manage any resulting business relationship and keep proportionate records of that correspondence. Depending on the context, this is generally necessary to respond to your request or supports our legitimate interests in managing business communications.

Form provider

The contact form is submitted through Formspree, which processes the form submission on our behalf. Do not put sensitive information in the form unless it is genuinely necessary.

Tracking and cookies

House of Carol does not currently use advertising trackers, analytics, fingerprinting or non-essential cookies on this website. External websites reached through links have their own practices.

Retention and sharing

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the enquiry, any resulting relationship and legitimate record-keeping. We do not sell enquiry information. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

Your rights and contact

UK data-protection law can give you rights over personal information, depending on the circumstances. To ask a privacy question or exercise a relevant right, use the House of Carol contact form and make clear that your message concerns privacy.
