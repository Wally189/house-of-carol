Privacy & UK GDPR

Use less data. Keep it only when it earns its place.

House of Carol is designed around data minimisation. This notice explains what personal information is used, why, and the choices people have.

Who is responsible

The controller is Alan Gallagher, sole trader, trading as House of Carol, based in Bristol, United Kingdom. The full service address is supplied on formal business correspondence and transaction documents where it is required. It is not published as general marketing information.

The public enquiry form on the current website is inactive, so House of Carol is not collecting enquiry submissions through this site at present. This notice will be updated with the permanent House contact route before live enquiry collection begins.

What information may be used

When the service is live, the normal information needed to handle an enquiry or customer job may include:

  • business-contact name and contact details;
  • the organisation represented and correspondence with House of Carol;
  • scope, order and service-delivery information;
  • invoice, payment and necessary business records;
  • feedback or complaint information; and
  • limited information contained in process evidence where it cannot reasonably be removed.

Customers are asked not to send passwords, credentials, health information, special-category data or unrelated third-party records. Process evidence should be redacted wherever practical.

Why it may be used

Enquiries and pre-contract discussions

To respond to a request and decide whether House of Carol can provide the service. The lawful basis will normally be steps requested before entering a contract, or legitimate interests for ordinary business-to-business administration where appropriate.

Customer work

To agree scope, communicate, invoice, deliver the service, answer clarification questions and deal with problems. The lawful basis will normally be contract for an individual contracting customer, or legitimate interests where a contact acts for an organisation.

Business, tax and compliance records

To meet legal obligations and maintain necessary business records.

Marketing

Any direct marketing will be separately checked against the applicable UK GDPR and PECR rules. Objections to direct marketing will be respected.

Sharing and technology

House of Carol may use proportionate suppliers for website hosting, email, document storage and business administration. The live enquiry and email stack will be named and checked before customer data is routed through it. Personal information is not sold.

Identifiable customer process evidence is kept out of AI-assisted analysis wherever practical through redaction or synthetic examples. House of Carol does not use solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about individuals.

Retention

  • Tax and transaction records are retained for the period required by HMRC.
  • Working process evidence is normally deleted within 30 days after final delivery and completion of the included clarification round, unless there is a documented reason to keep it longer.
  • Unsuccessful enquiries are normally deleted after six months unless needed for a dispute, legal obligation or another documented business reason.
  • A minimal suppression record may be retained when somebody opts out of marketing so that preference can be respected.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, people may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to processing, and exercise other rights under UK data-protection law. Once the permanent House contact route is live, requests can be made through that route.

If a concern is not resolved, a complaint can also be made to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
