Scope
One bounded complaints/redress process design; exact timing and effort confirmed before commitment.

House of Carol designs the process; it does not act as the customer's ombudsman, regulator or adjudicator.

What needs to be in place?

Customer policy and sector context.

Appropriate independence for material cases.

An accountable owner for the future complaints process.

What is outside the scope?

No ombudsman, regulator or adjudicative impersonation.

No legal determination of rights or liabilities.

Is this the right service?

It fits an organisation whose complaint handling is inconsistent, hard to trace or poor at turning cases into learning.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No legal correctness, regulator acceptance or particular complaint outcome is guaranteed. The customer retains authority for actual cases and remedies.
