What needs to be in place?

Appropriate customer fit; accurate tool/workflow information; current legal/privacy/security sources; customer participation.

What is outside the scope?

Legal opinion; DPO function; compliance certification; DPIA sign-off; unnecessary raw personal/confidential data.

Is this the right service for our organisation?

It is intended for small UK organisations using GenAI where staff or managers need practical, consistent rules about what information may be used with which tools.

Fit, competence, risk and the minimum necessary information are checked before any commitment.
