WORKED EXAMPLE

From “could AI help?” to one bounded human-operated workflow

An illustration of how a low-consequence recurring task can be assessed before implementation, then turned into a tested working method if the evidence supports it.

Illustrative example — shown to explain how the service can be applied. It is not a customer testimonial or measured result.

Situation

A recurring first draft takes time, but judgement still matters.

Example situation: a small B2B service business prepares a short internal account brief before client review meetings. The information comes from approved internal source documents, but the structure varies and experienced staff spend time assembling the first draft.

The task remains low consequence only because the output is a draft for a named human to verify. It is not suitable for unattended external sending or automatic decisions.

Illustrative current workflow
Gather approved source material
Read and extract key points
Draft account brief
Manager checks facts and judgement
Use in meeting preparation

What House of Carol would look at

First decide whether AI is the right intervention.

Clear purpose

The task produces a defined internal first draft rather than a final decision or external communication.

Known sources

The workflow can use a bounded set of approved source material rather than open-ended research.

Human authority

A named manager remains responsible for checking facts, context and judgement before the brief is used.

Manual fallback

The existing drafting method remains available if the AI workflow is unavailable, unsuitable or produces a weak result.

What a clearer state could look like

Turn the use case into a repeatable method, not a pile of prompts.

A bounded implementation would define the inputs, instructions, source priority, verification checks, human decision point, exception route and fallback before testing representative cases.

Illustrative target workflow
Human selects approved sources
Run defined AI workflow
AI produces structured draft
Human verifies facts and context
Revise or fall back if needed
Manager approves use

What the customer could receive

A controlled workflow another team member can actually run.
