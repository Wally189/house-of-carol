How a venture earns investment

Demand, economics, repeatability, fit.

Demand: credible evidence that somebody has the problem or desire.

Economics: a believable route to positive contribution and cash.

Repeatability: the work can become easier, faster or more reusable.

Fit: the venture can be delivered lawfully, competently and without breaking the House.
