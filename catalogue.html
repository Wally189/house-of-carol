Start where you are

Choose the right stage of help — not the biggest service.

Some customers need help deciding what the real problem is. Others already know exactly what needs changing. The catalogue is designed so you can enter at the right point rather than paying for diagnosis you do not need or implementation you are not ready for.

1 · Diagnose & decide

Work out what is worth fixing first.

Choose a review when the problem crosses several workflows, people or channels, or when you need evidence before deciding what to implement.

AI Workflow Opportunity Review
Customer Journey & Service Operations Review
Research & evidence services

2 · Implement & improve

Put a defined change into working use.

When the need is already clear, go directly to a bounded implementation, process, knowledge, training or completion service.

AI Workflow Implementation Sprint
SOP & Process Design Sprint
Website Completion Sprint

3 · Support & assure

Keep good work usable, controlled and trustworthy.

After implementation, use recurring support or focused assurance only where the work genuinely needs continuing attention or independent challenge.

AI Adoption Support Retainer
Evidence & Marketing Claim Substantiation Review
Tender Review

No forced sequence. Each service can stand on its own where it fits. A follow-on service is recommended only when the previous work shows there is a useful next step.
