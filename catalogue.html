Services

Start with the problem, not the package.

These are the kinds of work we can currently scope and test. We do not pretend every problem needs software, a transformation programme or a twelve-month retainer.

01

Website release check

A practical second pair of eyes before a website goes live or is handed to a client.

We agree the important customer journeys, exercise them in the relevant environments, record reproducible problems and retest the fixes. The point is not to generate a large audit. It is to find the things worth fixing before customers do.

Good fit
Agencies and small teams approaching launch or handover.
You receive
A prioritised findings record with evidence, impact, action and retest status.
Boundary
It is not formal accessibility certification, penetration testing or a substitute for specialist regulated assurance where that is required.

02

Data clean-up and structure

Turn inconsistent files into usable information without filling gaps by guesswork.

We can help with awkward spreadsheets, supplier files, catalogue records and structured extraction from documents. Rules are agreed first. Ambiguous items go to an exception list rather than being quietly invented.

Good fit
Small teams spending too much time correcting recurring data mess.
You receive
Cleaned output, rules used, exception list and a clear record of anything still uncertain.
Boundary
Accuracy claims are limited to what has actually been checked.

03

Workflow fix

When customers, jobs or information keep falling between the cracks, fix the crack.

We look at what actually happens between forms, inboxes, spreadsheets and people. The first answer may be a clearer hand-off, a better template or a small configuration change. Automation comes later if it earns its keep.

Good fit
Small organisations with a recurring operational nuisance that is costing time, enquiries or reliability.
You receive
A bounded fix, a simple operating route and evidence that the failure has been reduced or removed.
Boundary
No software build merely because software can be built.

04

Research for a decision

Research should make a decision easier, not make the document thicker.

We start with the question you need answered, use appropriate sources, distinguish evidence from inference and show what could change the conclusion. We stop when the uncertainty has been reduced enough for the decision in front of you.

Good fit
Founders and small teams deciding whether to pursue a market, supplier, opportunity or course of action.
You receive
A concise evidence-led answer, sources, limitations and the sensible next step.
Boundary
Where the question needs regulated legal, financial or other professional advice, we say so rather than imitating it.

If the sensible answer is “don’t buy this from us”, that is still a useful answer.

Not sure where it fits?

Describe the nuisance.

You do not need to diagnose the department, technology or service first. Tell us what keeps going wrong and what you need to be different.
