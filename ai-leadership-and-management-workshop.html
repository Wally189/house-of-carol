What changes

The management team moves from broad AI interest to explicit decisions about opportunities, risks, ownership, boundaries and next actions.

The workshop does not pretend that every organisation needs the same AI strategy or that every possible use should progress.

What needs to be in place?

Management participation.

Current business context.

What is outside the scope?

No generic executive-theatre product.

No legal or compliance certification.

No invented price, ROI or maturity claim.

Is this the right service?

It is intended for SME leaders and managers who need to make practical AI adoption, governance, risk and investment decisions.

If the need is implementation, policy drafting, baseline staff training or specialist regulated advice, a different route is likely to fit better.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No adoption, productivity, investment-return or compliance outcome is guaranteed. Leaders retain the decisions and competent specialist advice remains with the appropriate owner.
