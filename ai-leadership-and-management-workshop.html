Equip leaders to identify worthwhile AI uses, set boundaries, govern adoption and create an actionable next-step plan.

Leaders need practical capability to make AI adoption, governance, risk and investment decisions.

What changes

The management team moves from broad AI interest to explicit decisions about opportunities, risks, ownership, boundaries and next actions.

The workshop does not pretend that every organisation needs the same AI strategy or that every possible use should progress.

What you will receive

Leadership briefing

A focused briefing for the agreed management context.

Opportunity and risk framework

Governance decisions

Prioritisation exercise

Action plan

A practical next-step plan linked to the decisions made.

WORKED EXAMPLE

SEE HOW THIS CAN WORK

Example situation: a small leadership team has six possible AI ideas, mixed levels of enthusiasm and no agreed way to decide which opportunities are worth testing or what governance boundaries should apply.

House of Carol would use the workshop to frame the opportunity and risk questions, clarify decision rights, prioritise the candidate uses and turn the discussion into a bounded action plan rather than a generic AI strategy exercise.

A clearer target state: leaders can distinguish worthwhile opportunities from weak ones, know who owns the relevant decisions and have an explicit next-step plan.

The customer could receive: a leadership briefing, opportunity/risk framework, governance decisions, prioritisation exercise and action plan.
