Fixed-price work only works when the boundary is honest. House of Carol should decline or re-scope work rather than squeeze a much bigger problem into £495.

  • owner-led UK service business;
  • recurring quoted, agreed or completed work;
  • fragmented hand-offs into invoicing/payment visibility;
  • existing email, spreadsheets, shared drives or accounting tools that can be improved;

Not this service

  • bookkeeping or accounting;
  • tax or financial advice;
