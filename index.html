Independent British venture business.

Build useful things. Keep what works.

House of Carol develops, tests and operates commercial ventures around real problems. Some become services, products, publications, tools or other income-producing businesses. Others are changed or stopped when the evidence says they should be.

The House view

The parent business is stable. The ventures are allowed to change.

House of Carol exists to create lawful, useful and increasingly dependable income by developing ventures carefully rather than pretending every good idea is already a business.

What House of Carol does

One House. Different ways to create value.

We look for problems worth solving, investigate the people and markets around them, develop the smallest credible proposition and test whether it deserves further investment.

A venture may earn through client work, products, intellectual property, media, licensing, partnerships, owned assets or another lawful commercial model. The route depends on the opportunity rather than a fixed technology or business-fashion doctrine.

Only ventures developed far enough to explain responsibly appear on this website.

How the portfolio works

Ⅰ

Observe

Start with a real problem, unmet need, inefficiency, audience or commercial opportunity.

Ⅱ

Research

Separate an attractive idea from evidence about customers, alternatives, constraints and economics.

Ⅲ

Test

Put the smallest credible proposition in contact with reality and learn from what happens.

Ⅳ

Operate or stop

Useful ventures earn further investment. Weak ones change, wait or end. Capability alone is not a reason to continue.

Current position

The specialist businesses sit beneath the House.

Customers should meet a proposition that makes sense for the problem they are trying to solve, rather than navigate House of Carol's internal operating machinery.

As ventures mature, specialist customer-facing businesses can have their own focused websites while remaining clearly operated by House of Carol.
