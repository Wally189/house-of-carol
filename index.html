Fix what wastes time. Check what cannot afford to fail.

House of Carol currently works on two specific problems: recurring business processes that are slow, duplicated or unreliable, and websites that need an independent check before launch or handover.

What you can expect

A bounded piece of work, not an open-ended transformation programme.

We establish what is happening, agree what needs to change or be checked, do the defined work, test the important result and make the limits visible.

Recognise the problem?

Start with something observable.

Perhaps the same information is copied between systems, an approval routinely disappears, a hand-off depends on one person, or a process takes far longer than it should.

Or perhaps a website looks finished but nobody independent has checked the mobile journey, links, forms, enlarged text, accessibility basics, metadata and release-critical pages together.

Those are problems we can define, inspect and test.

Current service · 01Workflow implementationWe map one existing recurring process, remove avoidable friction, implement an agreed improvement where feasible, test it and hand it back with clear operating notes.Current service · 02Website release assuranceWe independently inspect agreed pages and one important user journey, record material defects with evidence and provide a release recommendation and bounded retest.

What the work looks like

Ⅰ

Show us the real thing

The current process, site, materials and intended result come before theories about solutions.

Ⅱ

Agree the boundary

Scope, exclusions, access, acceptance and important risks are made visible before material work begins.

Ⅲ

Make or inspect

We either implement the bounded improvement or perform the agreed independent release check.

Ⅳ

Test and hand back

You receive the result, evidence, known limitations and a clear next step rather than hidden dependency.

What House of Carol is

The current services are the beginning, not the definition of the company.

House of Carol is an owner-operated British venture business. It develops commercial ideas, tests whether they solve a real problem and keeps, changes or stops them according to evidence.

The two services on this site are the propositions currently developed far enough to describe. We do not publish a speculative catalogue of things we might someday sell.
