We build businesses.
Most ideas don’t survive the front door.

House of Carol is a British venture house. We turn useful ideas into services, tools and digital products, then test them against real people, real behaviour and real money.

Nothing is for sale here yet. We would rather show you a venture when it has earned the right to exist than pretend a half-built idea is a business.

What belongs here

Useful things with a reason to exist.

Some ideas become a service. Some become a tool, a product or a small standalone venture. Some get killed quickly because a cheaper substitute already does the job.

That is the point. House of Carol is not an idea museum. If something cannot create genuine value for somebody outside the House, it does not get promoted merely because we can build it.

Useful first
Start with a real job, problem or desire. Technology has to earn its place.
Evidence over theatre
No invented traction, fake testimonials or internal applause dressed up as demand.
Small before scale
Prove something cheaply and clearly before adding complexity, spend or grand plans.

The rule

No AI sludge. No busywork disguised as innovation. No keeping an idea alive because we are fond of it.

AI is part of how House of Carol works internally. It is not a substitute for customer evidence, judgement, responsibility or taste.

When the first venture is ready for the public, this page will point to something useful rather than another paragraph explaining how ambitious we are.
