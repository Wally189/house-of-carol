How it works

Clear scope. Clear evidence. Clear next steps.

We establish the real problem, make the important distinctions and leave a usable result. Known facts are separated from assumptions, and unresolved dependencies are stated rather than hidden.

Technology is used where it genuinely improves the work. It is not a substitute for judgement, and it is not the thing the customer is expected to buy.
