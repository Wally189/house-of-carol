What you can expect

Clear scope. Clear evidence. Clear next steps.

Scope, uncertainty and proof should stay visible throughout the work. Known facts are separated from assumptions, and unresolved dependencies are stated rather than hidden.

Technology is used where it genuinely improves the work. It is not a substitute for judgement, and it is not the thing the customer is expected to buy.

If the House is not the right fit for the job, saying so is a valid outcome.
