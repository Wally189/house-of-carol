Internal Academy / Learning Pathway Design

Design a coherent internal learning system from foundation to applied capability.

Learning is fragmented and not connected to capability progression or business outcomes.

For: Growing organisations.

£2,000 standard — indicative scope band £1,500–£3,000. Final price is confirmed after the bounded pathway scope is agreed.

A defined professional service centred on curriculum architecture, pathways, modules, practice, assessment, governance and a maintenance plan.

Defined capability and learner groups · content ownership required · no live academy operation or regulated qualification claim

Discuss the outcome you need

What changes

Fragmented learning is organised into a coherent pathway connecting the organisation’s defined capabilities, learner groups, practice and assessment.

The work designs the internal learning system and its governance; it does not claim to provide a degree, regulated qualification or safeguarding-sensitive delivery without the necessary authority and controls.

What you will receive

Curriculum architecture

A structured view of how the agreed capabilities and learning content fit together from foundation through to applied capability.

Pathways

Defined routes through the learning system for the agreed learner groups, connecting appropriate stages of development rather than presenting one undifferentiated curriculum.

Modules

Bounded learning units organised within the pathway so each part has a clear place in the wider capability system.

Practice

Applied activities designed to help learners use the agreed capability rather than relying on information transfer alone.

Assessment

Proportionate checks aligned to the intended learning and capability progression, without implying regulated certification.

Governance

Clear ownership for the learning content and decisions needed to keep the internal academy coherent and controlled.

Maintenance plan

A practical plan identifying how the organisation will own, review and update the pathway after handover; no unsupported update cadence is invented.
