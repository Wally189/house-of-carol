Customer Support Knowledge Base Build

Create a clear, maintainable knowledge base for the questions your team answers again and again.

When answers are scattered across documents, inboxes and individual staff knowledge, support becomes harder to deliver consistently.

We organise your approved information into a clear knowledge base, with clear ownership and a route for questions that need further judgement.

For: SMEs dealing with recurring customer questions, where answers are scattered, inconsistent or dependent on individual staff knowledge.

What changes

Repeated questions stop depending on whoever happens to know the answer.

Your team gets an organised set of approved answers, a clear route for questions that need further judgement, and named responsibility for keeping the information up to date.

What you will receive

Knowledge base

A structured set of priority support content for the agreed area of customer support.

Topic structure

A practical way to organise questions and answers so staff can find the right information more easily.

Answer and article standard

A consistent format for producing clear, usable support articles and approved answers.

Escalation route

A defined route for questions that are unclear, disputed, unknown or need specialist judgement.

Maintenance process

A practical process for reviewing, correcting, updating and retiring support content.

Ownership and handover

Clear responsibility for maintaining the knowledge base, including review roles, update triggers and handover to your team.

Final corrections

One round of agreed factual corrections before the project closes.

Scope
One agreed area of customer support, normally covering up to 30 source documents, pages or other key information sources and up to 15 priority articles or approved answers.

The project includes reviewing the source material, organising the knowledge base, agreeing a consistent answer format, defining escalation routes, assigning ownership, establishing a maintenance process and handing the completed structure over to your team.

Larger or more complex knowledge bases are scoped separately.

We confirm the final scope, delivery plan and project fee with you before work begins.

What needs to be in place?

You will need to provide the information your team currently uses to answer the agreed support questions and nominate someone who can confirm what is correct when information is unclear or conflicting.

We will also need timely approval of the final support content.

If the source information is unclear or incomplete, we will flag it rather than make assumptions.

What isn’t included?

The project does not include:

  • unlimited content migration;
  • ongoing House of Carol-managed maintenance or support after handover;
  • chatbot or AI deployment unless separately agreed;
  • replacement of legal, regulatory or other specialist advice or judgement;
  • invention of product facts, policies, promises or eligibility decisions;
  • publication of your content without your approval.

If you need additional content, systems work or ongoing support, that would need to be agreed separately.

Is this the right service?

This service may be a good fit if your organisation answers the same customer questions repeatedly and:

  • staff rely on different documents or individual knowledge;
  • answers vary depending on who responds;
  • nobody is clearly responsible for keeping support information current;
  • difficult or unknown questions do not have a clear escalation route.

If the main problem is something different — such as replacing a support platform, deploying a chatbot, redesigning the whole customer-service operation or producing a very large content library — this may not be the right service.

What if the work is larger than this?

If your source material, article volume, systems work or support area is substantially larger or more complex, we will identify that during scoping and agree a different scope with you before you commit.

Start with the repeated question

Which customer questions are being answered again and again?

Tell us about the support activity, the sources your team currently uses and where answers start to become inconsistent.

We can then establish whether this service fits and confirm the scope and project fee before you commit.
