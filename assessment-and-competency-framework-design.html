Discuss the outcome you need

What changes

The organisation gets a defined way to describe the capability it needs and a proportionate method for gathering and judging evidence of that capability.

The framework is bounded to the defined role or capability and does not imply a regulated qualification, licence to practise or high-stakes credential unless competent authority exists.

What you will receive

Competency model

A structured description of the defined capability so the organisation can distinguish what competent performance needs to cover.

Assessment criteria

Clear criteria aligned to the competency model, setting out what evidence should demonstrate against the agreed capability.

Evidence methods

Proportionate ways to gather evidence of performance or understanding, consistent with the agreed role, context and identity requirements.

Moderation approach

A method for checking that the framework is applied consistently rather than allowing materially different judgements for comparable evidence.

Scoring guidance

Practical guidance for applying the agreed criteria and recording assessment decisions consistently within the defined framework.

What needs to be in place?

A defined role or capability.

Appropriate evidence and identity requirements.

What is outside the scope?

No regulated qualification, licence-to-practise or high-stakes credential claim without authority.

Anything outside the agreed capability and assessment boundary becomes a separate decision.

Is this the right service?

This service fits organisations that need a proportionate way to define and assess a bounded capability.

If the assessment determines regulated status or requires specialist authority beyond House of Carol’s competence, a different route is required.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No qualification, licence, certification, learner-performance or business outcome is implied. Internal design evidence is not represented as external market proof.
