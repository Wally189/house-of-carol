Consultation & Evidence Synthesis Service

Provide structured synthesis that distinguishes themes, contrary evidence, limitations and decision implications.

Large consultation or evidence sets are difficult to analyse consistently and transparently.

Discuss the question

What changes

A large response or evidence set becomes a transparent synthesis that shows the main themes, meaningful contrary evidence and what the data cannot support.

The service does not manufacture respondent evidence or claim representativeness beyond the supplied data and method.

What you will receive

Coding approach

A documented, proportionate method for organising the supplied responses or evidence against the agreed research question.

Evidence synthesis

A traceable synthesis that distinguishes what the data supports from interpretation, inference and unanswered questions.

Themes

Material themes grounded in the supplied evidence, with frequency or prominence described only where the data allows it.

Contrary evidence

Meaningful disagreement, minority positions and evidence that cuts across the dominant themes kept visible rather than averaged away.

Limitations

A clear account of data quality, scope, missing information and any limits on representativeness or interpretation.

Source/data appendix

A structured appendix showing how material findings connect back to the supplied source or data set.
