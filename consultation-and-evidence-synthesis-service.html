What needs to be in place?

Lawful access to responses/data; defined research question; methodology.

What is outside the scope?

No fabrication of respondent evidence; no claim of representativeness beyond data.

How are disagreement and limitations handled?

Contrary evidence and limitations remain visible in the synthesis. The analysis is not designed to manufacture unanimity or a conclusion the underlying evidence cannot support.
