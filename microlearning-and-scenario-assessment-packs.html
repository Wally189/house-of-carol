What changes

A defined topic is turned into short learning and realistic scenarios that require learners to exercise judgement rather than merely click through content.

The current price applies to a commissioned project. Any reusable paid licence or reuse model remains a separate commercial decision.

What you will receive

Microlearning modules

Short, focused learning units built around the authorised topic and current source material, designed to prepare the learner for the scenario judgements that follow.

Workplace scenarios

Realistic work-style situations that ask learners to apply the topic in context rather than repeat information from the learning material.

Answer rationales

Explanations showing why the available responses are stronger or weaker against the agreed authoritative content and intended judgement.

Assessment

A proportionate check of understanding or judgement aligned to the defined topic, without representing completion as accredited certification.

Facilitator/manager notes

Supporting notes for the agreed use of the pack, helping the organisation discuss the scenarios and interpret the learning within its own operating context.
