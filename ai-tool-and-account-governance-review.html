AI Tool & Account Governance Review

Provide a clear inventory and governance view of how AI tools and accounts should be controlled within the organisation.

Multiple tools and accounts create uncertainty about data handling, permissions, ownership and appropriate use.

For: Organisations using several AI services or account types.

£1,250 standard — indicative scope band £750–£2,000. Final price is confirmed after scope fit.

One bounded organisation or business-unit review covering the tool/account inventory, use and account classification, control gaps, priority actions and escalation list.

Governance-level review · no credentials required · no penetration testing, certification or legal advice

Discuss the review

What changes

The organisation gets a clear view of which AI tools and account types exist, how they are being used, where ownership or control is weak and which actions matter first.

Vendor documentation and the customer's own policy context remain visible sources rather than assumptions.

What you will receive

Tool/account inventory

A structured inventory of the tools and account types in scope.

Use classification

A practical classification of the uses and account arrangements identified.

Control-gap analysis

Material gaps in ownership, permissions, evidence or operating control.

Priority actions

A focused set of actions ordered by importance and dependency.

Escalation list

Items that need specialist or customer-authority decisions rather than invented answers.

What needs to be in place?

Accurate account and tool data.

Current vendor documentation.

The customer's relevant policy context.

Passwords, MFA codes and other credentials are not required for the standard review.

What is outside the scope?

Not penetration testing.

Not cybersecurity certification.

Not legal advice.

Is this the right service?

It is designed for organisations using several AI services or account types where ownership, use, data handling or account control is unclear.

If the issue is a deep technical security test, a legal determination or a single simple account question, a different route is more proportionate.

What does House of Carol not promise?

No certification, legal clearance or guarantee that every technical vulnerability has been identified is implied.
